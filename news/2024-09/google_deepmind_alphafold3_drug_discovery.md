## Google DeepMind's Latest AI Model Poised to Revolutionize Drug Discovery

**Source:** Time  
**Date:** 2024-09-15  
**URL:** https://time.com/6975934/google-deepmind-alphafold-3-ai/?utm_source=openai  
**Summary:** Google DeepMind introduced AlphaFold 3, a model predicting the structure and interactions of biological molecules, greatly accelerating biological research and drug discovery.

---

### What Happened

Google DeepMind unveiled AlphaFold 3, an advanced AI model capable of predicting the structures and interactions of a wide range of biological molecules, including proteins, DNA, RNA, and small molecules. This development is expected to significantly accelerate biological research and drug discovery processes. 

### Why It Matters

AlphaFold 3's ability to accurately model molecular interactions is poised to revolutionize drug discovery by enabling researchers to predict how drugs interact with target proteins more efficiently. This advancement could lead to faster development of new treatments and a deeper understanding of various diseases. 

### Who's Involved

- **Google DeepMind:** Developed AlphaFold 3, building upon previous iterations to enhance the prediction of molecular structures and interactions.
- **Isomorphic Labs:** A subsidiary of Google DeepMind, applying AlphaFold 3 in drug design to accelerate and improve the success of drug development. 

### Technical Details

- **Model:** AlphaFold 3, an AI system designed to predict the structures and interactions of biological molecules.
- **Capabilities:** Predicts the structures of proteins, DNA, RNA, and small molecules, and models their interactions, including protein-ligand binding.
- **Architecture:** Utilizes an improved version of the Evoformer module and a diffusion network to assemble predictions. 

---

### Benchmark Results

AlphaFold 3 has demonstrated superior performance in predicting protein-ligand interactions compared to traditional methods. It is 50% more accurate than the best traditional methods on the PoseBusters benchmark without requiring structural information, making it the first AI system to surpass physics-based tools for biomolecular structure prediction.

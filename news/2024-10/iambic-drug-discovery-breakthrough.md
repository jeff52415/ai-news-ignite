## Nvidia-Backed AI Firm Iambic Unveils Drug Discovery Breakthrough

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** October 29, 2024  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/)
**Summary:** Iambic Therapeutics, a biotech firm supported by Nvidia, introduced "Enchant," an AI model trained on extensive pre-clinical data to predict early-stage drug performance, potentially reducing drug development costs and failure rates.

---

### What Happened
Iambic Therapeutics unveiled "Enchant," an AI model designed to predict early-stage drug performance. Trained on extensive pre-clinical data, Enchant achieved a 0.74 accuracy prediction score, surpassing previous models with a 0.58 score. This advancement could significantly reduce drug development costs and failure rates. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters
The introduction of Enchant addresses a critical bottleneck in drug discovery by leveraging abundant pre-clinical data to predict clinical outcomes. This approach aims to streamline the development process, potentially halving the time and investment required to bring new drugs to market. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved
- **Iambic Therapeutics:** A clinical-stage biotechnology company developing novel therapeutics using an AI-driven discovery platform.
- **Nvidia:** Provided investment and support for Iambic's AI initiatives.
- **Frances Arnold:** Nobel laureate and member of Iambic's board, highlighting the significance of Enchant in addressing challenges beyond structural predictions. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/?utm_source=openai))

### Technical Details
- **Model:** Enchant, a multi-modal transformer model.
- **Training Data:** Large troves of pre-clinical data derived from laboratory tests.
- **Performance Benchmark:** Achieved a 0.74 accuracy prediction score for human pharmacokinetics, setting a new benchmark compared to previous models with a 0.58 score. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/nvidia-backed-ai-firm-iambic-unveils-drug-discovery-breakthrough-2024-10-29/?utm_source=openai))

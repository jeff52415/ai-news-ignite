## DeepSeek Releases AI Model Upgrade, Intensifying Competition

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** March 25, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.gizchina.com/news/deepseek-unveils-ai-model-deepseek-v3-0324](https://www.gizchina.com/news/deepseek-unveils-ai-model-deepseek-v3-0324)  
**Summary:** Chinese AI startup DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V3-0324, an upgraded large language model with improved reasoning and coding capabilities. The release heightens competition with US tech leaders OpenAI and Anthropic.

---

### What Happened

DeepSeek, a Chinese AI startup, unveiled DeepSeek-V3-0324, an enhanced version of its large language model. The model is available for free download on Hugging Face, licensed under the MIT License, and is open for both personal and commercial use.

### Why It Matters

This release signifies DeepSeek's commitment to advancing AI technology and its strategic move to compete with established U.S. tech giants like OpenAI and Anthropic. By offering a powerful, free alternative, DeepSeek aims to democratize AI access and challenge the dominance of Western companies in the AI sector.

### Who's Involved

- **DeepSeek:** A Chinese AI startup responsible for developing and releasing the DeepSeek-V3-0324 model.
- **OpenAI:** A leading U.S. AI research organization, now facing intensified competition from DeepSeek.
- **Anthropic:** Another U.S.-based AI company that may experience increased competition due to DeepSeek's advancements.

### Technical Details

- **Model Name:** DeepSeek-V3-0324
- **Parameters:** 685 billion
- **Architecture:** Mixture of Experts (MoE)
- **Active Parameters:** Only 37 billion are active at any time, enhancing efficiency.
- **Innovations:**
  - **Multi-Head Latent Attention (MLA):** Improves context maintenance in long texts.
  - **Multi-Token Prediction (MTP):** Allows generation of multiple tokens simultaneously, increasing output speed by 80%.
- **Hardware Compatibility:** Runs efficiently on consumer hardware, including Mac Studio with Apple's M3 Ultra chip.
- **License:** MIT License, available for free download on Hugging Face.

### Benchmark Results

Benchmark tests have shown enhanced performance across multiple technical metrics, including improved reasoning and coding capabilities compared to its predecessor.

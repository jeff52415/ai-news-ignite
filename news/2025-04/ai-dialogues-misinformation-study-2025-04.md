## 📰 Study Finds AI Dialogues Reduce Misinformation Beliefs Without Building Lasting Discernment Skills

**Source:** arxiv.org  
**Date:** 2025-04-01  
**URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06517?utm_source=openai](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06517?utm_source=openai)  

**Summary:** Research indicates that while AI-assisted dialogues help participants better identify visual misinformation in the moment, these gains do not persist when faced with new misinformation, suggesting limited lasting learning benefits from current AI interventions.

# 📰 Berkeley Lab Builds AI-Guided Automated Laboratory

**Source:** Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory  
**Date:** 2025-04-29  
**URL:** [https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2025/04/29/harnessing-artificial-intelligence-for-high-impact-science/](https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2025/04/29/harnessing-artificial-intelligence-for-high-impact-science/)

Berkeley Lab is creating an automated laboratory with AI-guided robotics to expedite material development and testing processes, accelerating innovations in technologies like fuel cells and batteries.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Berkeley Lab is developing an automated laboratory, known as A-Lab, that utilizes AI-guided robotics to accelerate the discovery and testing of new materials. This system aims to expedite the development of materials for technologies such as fuel cells and batteries.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The traditional process of testing and validating new materials is time-consuming and labor-intensive. By integrating AI and automation, A-Lab can process 50 to 100 times more samples daily than human researchers, significantly speeding up the material discovery process. This advancement is crucial for accelerating innovations in clean energy technologies.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Berkeley Lab:** Leading the development of A-Lab, integrating AI and robotics into material science research.
- **Yan Zeng:** Staff scientist at Berkeley Lab, leading the A-Lab project.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **A-Lab System:** An automated laboratory that uses AI to generate chemical recipes from scientific literature and data, which are then synthesized by robotic components.
- **Materials Project:** A database providing data for A-Lab's AI algorithms.
- **GNoME:** Google DeepMinds Generative Network of Materials Exploration, contributing data to A-Lab.

---

### 🔹 Benchmark Results

Specific benchmark results are not provided, but the technology is expected to increase sample throughput by 50 to 100 times compared to traditional manual processes.

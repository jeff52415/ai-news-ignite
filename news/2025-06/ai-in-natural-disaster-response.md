### 🔹 What Happened

The United Nations launched the Global Initiative on Resilience to Natural Hazards through AI Solutions, aiming to guide governments, organizations, and communities in utilizing AI for disaster management. This initiative builds upon previous efforts by the International Telecommunications Union, the World Meteorological Organization (WMO), and the UN Environment Programme to develop best practices for AI use in disaster management, including enhancing data collection, improving forecasting, and streamlining communications.  ([time.com](https://time.com/7171445/ai-natural-disaster-cities/))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

With urban populations increasing and the frequency of extreme weather events rising due to climate change, cities are more vulnerable to natural disasters. AI offers advanced tools to predict, monitor, and respond to these events, potentially saving lives and reducing economic losses. The UN's initiative aims to ensure that AI is used safely and effectively in managing natural hazards, promoting resilience in urban areas.  ([time.com](https://time.com/7171445/ai-natural-disaster-cities/))

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **United Nations**: Launched the Global Initiative on Resilience to Natural Hazards through AI Solutions.
- **International Telecommunications Union (ITU)**: Collaborated with WMO and UNEP to develop best practices for AI in disaster management.
- **World Meteorological Organization (WMO)**: Partnered with ITU and UNEP in the initiative.
- **UN Environment Programme (UNEP)**: Collaborated with ITU and WMO in the initiative.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **AI Forecasting Models**: Utilized to predict hurricane landfall and other natural disasters.
- **Satellite Image Analysis**: Employed for post-disaster assessments.
- **AI Translation Services**: Implemented to deliver forecasts in multiple languages, reducing translation time from an hour to 10 minutes.  ([time.com](https://time.com/7171445/ai-natural-disaster-cities/))

## AI Accelerates Scientific Discovery

**Source:**   
**Date:** 2025-07-10  
**URL:** [https://time.com/7212504/time100-impact-awards-anima-anandkumar/?utm_source=openai](https://time.com/7212504/time100-impact-awards-anima-anandkumar/?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** Anima Anandkumar, a professor at Caltech, has developed innovative AI models called neural operators that simulate physical systems rapidly and accurately. These advances support breakthroughs in weather forecasting, nuclear fusion research, and medical device design, significantly speeding up scientific discovery and problem solving across multiple disciplines.

---

### What Happened
Anima Anandkumar of Caltech developed neural operator AI models that efficiently simulate complex physical phenomena. These models dramatically accelerate simulations used in weather forecasting, plasma behavior in nuclear fusion, and designing safer medical devices.

### Why It Matters
Traditional physical simulations can be computationally expensive and slow, limiting research progress. Anandkumar's neural operators enable fast, scalable, and precise simulations, allowing scientists and engineers to iterate quickly and advance innovations in critical areas such as climate prediction, fusion energy, and healthcare.

### Who's Involved
- **Anima Anandkumar:** Professor at Caltech and developer of neural operator AI.
- **European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF):** Collaborator using FourCastNet, a neural operator-based weather model.
- **UK Atomic Energy Agency:** Collaborator applying neural operators in nuclear fusion plasma modeling.

### Technical Details
- **Neural Operators:** A generalization of neural networks capable of learning mappings between infinite-dimensional function spaces, enabling simulation of physical systems with higher speed and resolution.
- **FourCastNet:** A neural operator-based model which produces high-resolution, fast weather forecasts (e.g., week-long forecasts in under two seconds).
- **Plasma Modeling for Nuclear Fusion:** Neural operators predict plasma states orders of magnitude faster than traditional solvers, aiding in reactor safety and efficiency.
- **Medical Device Design:** AI-driven design of catheters that reduce bacterial contamination by two orders of magnitude.

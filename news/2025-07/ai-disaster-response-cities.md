## 📰 How AI Is Being Used to Respond to Natural Disasters in Cities

**Source:** TIME  
**Date:** 2025-07-15  
**URL:** https://time.com/7171445/ai-natural-disaster-cities/?utm_source=openai  
**Summary:** AI technologies are enhancing urban responses to natural disasters by improving forecasting, monitoring, and coordination. Initiatives by the UN and private companies aim to integrate AI for better disaster prediction and management, though challenges in data quality and infrastructure persist.

---

### 🔹 What Happened
The United Nations launched the Global Initiative on Resilience to Natural Hazards through AI Solutions, focusing on guiding governments and organizations in utilizing AI for disaster management. This initiative builds upon previous efforts by the International Telecommunications Union, the World Meteorological Organization, and the UN Environment Programme to develop best practices for AI in disaster management. 

### 🔹 Why It Matters
As urban populations grow and climate change intensifies extreme weather events, cities face increased risks from natural disasters. Integrating AI into disaster response can enhance preparedness, improve response times, and potentially save lives and resources.

### 🔹 Who's Involved
- **United Nations**: Launched the Global Initiative on Resilience to Natural Hazards through AI Solutions.
- **International Telecommunications Union (ITU)**: Collaborated with other UN agencies to develop AI best practices for disaster management.
- **World Meteorological Organization (WMO)**: Partnered in creating AI guidelines for disaster response.
- **UN Environment Programme**: Contributed to the development of AI best practices for disaster management.

### 🔹 Technical Details
- **AI Applications in Disaster Management**:
  - **Forecasting**: AI models analyze historical data to predict natural disasters, such as hurricanes and floods.
  - **Monitoring**: AI processes satellite imagery to assess damage and identify affected areas.
  - **Coordination**: AI systems optimize resource allocation and communication during disaster response.

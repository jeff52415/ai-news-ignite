## 📰 AI's Impact on High-Growth Teams in Marketing

**Source:** GrowthLoop Blog  
**Date:** June 18, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.growthloop.com/post/new-study-reveals-impact-of-ai-on-high-performing-teams](https://www.growthloop.com/post/new-study-reveals-impact-of-ai-on-high-performing-teams)  
**Summary:** A recent study by GrowthLoop highlights the increasing adoption of AI in marketing, emphasizing its role in automating tasks like targeting, segmentation, and optimization. High-performing organizations leverage AI to enhance performance and speed, allowing marketers to concentrate on strategic initiatives and scalable business outcomes.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

GrowthLoop released a study revealing that while AI adoption in marketing is on the rise, many teams still face challenges in achieving rapid growth and efficiency. The research indicates that only 22% of marketers believe their current strategies are driving fast growth, and just 10% feel their teams execute at the necessary speed to succeed.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This study underscores the gap between the potential of AI and its current application in marketing. It highlights the need for organizations to integrate AI more deeply into their workflows to overcome bottlenecks and achieve faster, more efficient marketing cycles.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **GrowthLoop:** A pioneer in AI-driven marketing acceleration, conducting the study to assess AI's impact on marketing performance.
- **Ascend2:** Partnered with GrowthLoop to benchmark marketing teams' performance in the study.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **AI Integration:** High-performing organizations are embedding AI into their marketing workflows, automating critical tasks such as targeting, segmentation, and performance optimization.
- **Data Sources:** The study is based on responses from over 300 senior marketers across North America.

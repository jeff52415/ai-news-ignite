---

###  What Happened

In July 2025, Google DeepMind's Gemini Pro and OpenAI's O1 series AI models achieved gold medal status at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO). DeepMind's Gemini Pro officially participated in the competition, while OpenAI's O1 series models were evaluated using the same grading standards and achieved similar results.

###  Why It Matters

This milestone signifies a major advancement in AI's ability to perform complex mathematical reasoning tasks. The success of these models suggests that AI can now tackle sophisticated problems requiring multi-step, creative proofs, moving beyond mere pattern recognition. This development has potential applications in various fields, including science, engineering, and education.

###  Who's Involved

- **Google DeepMind:** Developed the Gemini Pro model, which officially participated in the IMO and achieved gold medal status.
- **OpenAI:** Developed the O1 series models, which were evaluated using the same grading standards and achieved similar results.

###  Technical Details

- **Models:**
  - **Gemini Pro:** An advanced AI model developed by Google DeepMind, capable of solving complex mathematical problems.
  - **O1 Series:** A series of AI models developed by OpenAI, designed for enhanced reasoning capabilities.

- **Methods:**
  - **Extended "Thinking Time":** Allowing the models more time to process and reason through problems.
  - **Multi-Agent Debate and Consensus-Building:** Techniques used to improve the models' problem-solving abilities.
  - **Reinforcement Learning Techniques:** New methods that reward clarity as well as correctness in the models' outputs.

- **Data:**
  - **IMO Problems:** The models were evaluated using problems from the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad.

---

###  Benchmark Results

Both models solved five out of six IMO problems, achieving a gold medal score of 35 out of a possible 42 points.

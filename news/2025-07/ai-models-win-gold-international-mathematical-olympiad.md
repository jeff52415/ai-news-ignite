## 📰 AI Models Win Gold at International Mathematical Olympiad

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** July 21, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-intelligencer-how-ai-won-math-gold-2025-07-24/ (original URL, currently inaccessible)](https://www.reuters.com/technology/ai-intelligencer-how-ai-won-math-gold-2025-07-24/)  
**Summary:** Google DeepMind's Gemini Deep Think and OpenAI's experimental reasoning model achieved gold-medal status at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) by solving five out of six complex problems, marking a significant advancement in AI's mathematical reasoning capabilities.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

In July 2025, Google DeepMind's Gemini Deep Think and OpenAI's experimental reasoning model both achieved gold-medal status at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO). DeepMind's model officially participated in the competition, solving five out of six problems and earning 35 points. OpenAI's model, while not officially entered, was evaluated using the same grading standards and achieved similar results.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This achievement signifies a substantial advancement in AI's ability to perform complex mathematical reasoning. The success of these models suggests that AI could soon assist in solving advanced, unsolved mathematical problems, potentially transforming fields such as science, engineering, and education.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Google DeepMind**: Developed the Gemini Deep Think model, which officially participated in the IMO and achieved gold-medal status.
- **OpenAI**: Developed the experimental reasoning model, which was evaluated on the IMO problems and achieved similar results to DeepMind's model.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Models**:
  - **Gemini Deep Think**: An advanced AI model developed by Google DeepMind, equipped with the Deep Think feature, which enhances its reasoning capabilities.
  - **Experimental Reasoning Model**: A model developed by OpenAI, known for its enhanced reasoning abilities.

- **Methods**:
  - **Deep Think**: A feature in Gemini Deep Think that allows the model to perform complex reasoning tasks by generating and evaluating multiple ideas simultaneously.
  - **Extended "Thinking Time"**: A strategy employed by both models, allowing them to spend additional time reasoning before providing answers.
  - **Multi-Agent Debate and Consensus-Building**: Techniques used to improve the models' problem-solving abilities by simulating discussions among multiple agents.
  - **Reinforcement Learning Techniques**: Methods that reward clarity as well as correctness, enhancing the models' reasoning processes.

- **Data**:
  - **IMO Problems**: The official problems from the 2025 International Mathematical Olympiad, which the models solved to achieve gold-medal status.

---

### 🔹 Benchmark Results

Both models achieved gold-medal status at the IMO by solving five out of six problems, earning 35 points. This performance is on par with the top human contestants, highlighting the models' advanced reasoning capabilities.

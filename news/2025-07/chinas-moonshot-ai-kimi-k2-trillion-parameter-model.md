## Summary

Moonshot AI announced the launch of its Kimi K2 model, an advanced large language model featuring 1 trillion parameters. This Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model was trained on an extensive dataset of 15.5 trillion tokens, showcasing exceptional performance benchmarks. The model also includes efficient agentic capabilities, highlighting significant progress in China's AI development and innovation.

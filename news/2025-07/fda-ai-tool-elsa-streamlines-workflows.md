## FDA Introduces 'Elsa,' an AI Tool to Streamline Regulatory Workflows

**Source:** Fladgate  
**Date:** 2025-07-01  
**URL:** [https://www.fladgate.com/insights/ai-round-up-july-2025](https://www.fladgate.com/insights/ai-round-up-july-2025)  
**Summary:** The FDA launched Elsa, a generative AI assisting employees in processing and synthesizing regulatory and scientific data efficiently to improve decision-making.

---

### What Happened  
The FDA has launched Elsa, a generative AI tool aimed at streamlining regulatory workflows by helping staff process and synthesize scientific and regulatory data more efficiently. Elsa supports clinical protocol reviews, adverse event summarization, scientific evaluations, and identifying inspection priorities. It operates in a secure environment to protect sensitive data and is designed to enhance decision-making and reduce review times.

### Why It Matters  
Elsa represents a significant modernization of the FDA's regulatory review process. By automating data synthesis and routine tasks, the AI tool allows scientists and regulators to focus their expertise where it is most needed, accelerating drug approvals and improving public health outcomes. This aligns the FDA with broader AI adoption trends in regulatory science and healthcare.

### Who's Involved  
- **FDA Commissioner Marty Makary**: Announced the successful rollout of Elsa.  
- **FDA Chief AI Officer Jeremy Walsh**: Highlighted Elsa's role in boosting staff effectiveness.  
- **FDA employees across centers**: Collaborated to implement Elsa ahead of schedule and budget.

### Technical Details  
- **Tool:** Elsa, a generative AI system tailored for regulatory science.  
- **Capabilities:**  
  - Summarizes adverse event reports.  
  - Assists clinical protocol review and scientific evaluation.  
  - Generates code for nonclinical data processing.  
  - Identifies priority inspection targets.  
- **Deployment:** Operates within a secure government cloud environment to protect confidentiality; no sensitive data is used to train models externally.  
- **Efficiency Gains:** Reduced some review tasks from 2-3 days to approximately 6 minutes.

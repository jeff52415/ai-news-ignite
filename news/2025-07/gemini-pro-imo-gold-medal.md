### 🔹 What Happened

In July 2025, Google DeepMind's advanced AI model, Gemini Pro, achieved a gold medal at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), solving five out of six complex mathematical problems. OpenAI's models also demonstrated similar capabilities, solving five out of six problems, though they did not officially participate in the competition.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This milestone signifies a substantial advancement in AI's ability to perform complex reasoning tasks, moving beyond mere pattern recognition. The success of Gemini Pro and OpenAI's models suggests potential applications in various fields, including scientific research, coding, and creative endeavors, where advanced problem-solving is essential.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Google DeepMind**: Developed the Gemini Pro model, which officially participated in the IMO and achieved gold medal status.
- **OpenAI**: Developed models that were evaluated on the IMO problems, achieving similar results to Gemini Pro.
- **International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)**: The organization that conducted the competition, officially grading and certifying Gemini Pro's performance.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Model**: Gemini Pro, an advanced version of Google's Gemini model, equipped with the Deep Think reasoning mode.
- **Methodology**: Utilized extended "thinking time," multi-agent debate, consensus-building, and new reinforcement learning techniques that reward clarity as well as correctness.
- **Data**: Trained on a curated corpus of high-quality solutions to mathematics problems, with access to general hints and tips on how to approach IMO problems.

## Germany Unveils AI Strategy Aiming to Drive 10% of GDP by 2030

**Summary:** Germany announced a comprehensive AI strategy to boost its economy by 2030 through initiatives like EU-funded AI gigafactories, quantum computing projects, and enhanced research commercialization to capture 10% GDP from AI.

---

### What Happened

Germany unveiled an ambitious AI strategy aiming to generate 10% of its economic output from AI by 2030. The plan includes establishing AI "gigafactories," advancing quantum computing, and enhancing research commercialization. The German cabinet is expected to approve the strategy by the end of July 2025.

### Why It Matters

This initiative underscores Germany's commitment to becoming a global leader in AI and quantum technologies, addressing competitive disadvantages and dependencies. By investing in AI infrastructure and research, Germany aims to bolster its economic growth and technological prowess.

### Who's Involved

- **German Government:** Leading the AI strategy and expected to approve the plan by the end of July 2025.
- **European Commission:** Allocated €20 billion for the construction of AI "gigafactories" across the EU.
- **Deutsche Telekom:** Investing in data centers and advocating for increased AI development in Europe.

### Technical Details

- **AI Gigafactories:** Large-scale AI processing centers to be constructed within the EU, with the first startup planned for mid-2027.
- **Quantum Computing:** Development of two "error-corrected quantum computers" by 2030 for broader use.
- **Research Commercialization:** Enhanced efforts to link AI research with industries such as robotics, mechanical engineering, automotive, and chemicals.

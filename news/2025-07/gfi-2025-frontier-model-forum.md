**Summary:** Leading AI companies joined to create the Frontier Model Forum, an industry body to promote safe and responsible development of frontier AI models by benchmarking, researching safety, collaborating with regulators, and applying AI to real-world problems.

---

###  What Happened

In July 2023, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, and OpenAI announced the formation of the Frontier Model Forum, an industry body focused on ensuring the safe and responsible development of frontier AI models. [Source](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/updates/announcing-the-frontier-model-forum/?utm_source=openai) The Forum aims to advance AI safety research, identify best practices, and facilitate information sharing among policymakers and industry stakeholders.

###  Why It Matters

The rapid advancement of AI technologies presents significant challenges related to safety, security, and ethical considerations. The Frontier Model Forum seeks to address these challenges by promoting responsible development and deployment of frontier AI models, ensuring they are aligned with societal values and do not pose undue risks.

###  Who's Involved

- **Anthropic**: An AI safety and research company.
- **Google**: A multinational technology company specializing in internet-related services and products.
- **Microsoft**: A multinational technology company known for its software products and services.
- **OpenAI**: An AI research organization focused on developing and promoting friendly AI.

###  Technical Details

The Frontier Model Forum focuses on:

- **Advancing AI Safety Research**: Promoting responsible development of frontier models and minimizing potential risks.
- **Identifying Best Practices**: Establishing safety standards and practices to mitigate a wide range of potential risks.
- **Facilitating Information Sharing**: Collaborating with policymakers, academics, civil society, and companies to share knowledge about trust and safety risks.
- **Supporting Societal Applications**: Developing applications that can help meet societys greatest challenges, such as climate change mitigation and adaptation, early cancer detection and prevention, and combating cyber threats.

Membership is open to organizations that develop and deploy frontier models, demonstrate a strong commitment to frontier model safety, and are willing to contribute to the Forums efforts. [Source](https://www.frontiermodelforum.org/updates/announcing-the-frontier-model-forum/?utm_source=openai)

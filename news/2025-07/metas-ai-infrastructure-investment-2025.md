### 🔹 What Happened

Meta Platforms CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced plans to invest hundreds of billions of dollars in AI data centers, including projects like the Prometheus data center and Hyperion, aiming to develop superintelligent systems.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This significant investment underscores Meta's commitment to advancing artificial intelligence and establishing a leadership position in the field. The development of large-scale AI data centers is crucial for training advanced AI models and supporting the computational demands of superintelligent systems.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Mark Zuckerberg**: CEO of Meta Platforms, leading the investment initiative.
- **Alexandr Wang**: Former CEO of Scale AI, recruited to lead Meta's Superintelligence Labs division.
- **Nat Friedman**: Former CEO of GitHub, also leading Meta's Superintelligence Labs division.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Prometheus Data Center**: Scheduled to launch in 2026, this data center is part of Meta's strategy to build massive AI data centers focused on developing superintelligent systems.
- **Hyperion Data Center**: A multi-gigawatt data center that Meta plans to build, with a footprint large enough to cover most of Manhattan.

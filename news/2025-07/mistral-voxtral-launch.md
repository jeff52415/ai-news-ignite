# Mistral Launches Voxtral: Open-Source Multilingual AI Audio Model

**Source:** MarketingProfs  
**Date:** 2025-07-18

French startup Mistral released Voxtral, an innovative open-weight speech model that supports multiple languages. Voxtral is offered in both large and lightweight versions, designed to efficiently perform transcription, summarization, and command execution tasks. This open-source model aims to provide accessible and versatile AI audio processing capabilities to a wide range of users and applications.

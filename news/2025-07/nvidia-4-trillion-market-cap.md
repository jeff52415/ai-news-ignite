# 📰 Nvidia's Market Capitalization Surpasses $4 Trillion

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** 2025-07-14  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/new-ai-benchmarks-test-speed-running-ai-applications-2025-04-02/](https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/new-ai-benchmarks-test-speed-running-ai-applications-2025-04-02/)  
**Summary:** Nvidia has become the first company to reach a market capitalization of $4 trillion, reflecting its dominant position in the AI industry.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

On July 9, 2025, Nvidia's stock price surged, briefly pushing its market capitalization past the $4 trillion mark, making it the first company to achieve this milestone. The stock closed at $164.10 per share, with a market cap of $3.97 trillion. ([washingtonpost.com](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/07/10/nvidia-4-trillion-market-cap/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This achievement underscores Nvidia's pivotal role in the artificial intelligence (AI) sector. The company's GPUs are integral to AI data centers, powering applications from machine learning to large language models. Nvidia's dominance in this space has attracted significant investments from major tech firms, further solidifying its market position. ([washingtonpost.com](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2025/07/10/nvidia-4-trillion-market-cap/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Nvidia Corporation**: The semiconductor company leading in AI hardware and software solutions.
- **Jensen Huang**: CEO of Nvidia, whose leadership has been instrumental in the company's growth.
- **Major Tech Firms**: Companies like Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, and Alphabet, which rely on Nvidia's GPUs for their AI infrastructure.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Graphics Processing Units (GPUs)**: Nvidia's specialized hardware essential for AI computations.
- **CUDA Software Platform**: Nvidia's parallel computing platform and application programming interface model.

# New York State Passes RAISE Act, Imposing AI Transparency Standards

**Source:** Fladgate  
**Date:** 2025-07-01  
**URL:** [https://www.fladgate.com/insights/ai-round-up-july-2025](https://www.fladgate.com/insights/ai-round-up-july-2025)  

## Summary

The Responsible AI Safety and Education (RAISE) Act has been passed by New York State, setting new legally binding requirements for transparency in AI systems categorized as high-risk. The legislation mandates organizations to disclose detailed risk assessments and implement strict safety protocols to minimize harm from AI operation.

Key provisions include:
- Comprehensive risk disclosure about the AI's potential impacts.
- Safety and mitigation measures for systems identified as potentially high-risk.
- Regular auditing and monitoring requirements to ensure ongoing compliance.
- Clear penalties and enforcement actions against entities that fail to meet the standards.

The RAISE Act represents a significant regulatory step aimed at fostering responsible AI development while protecting public interests. It compels increased accountability and transparency from developers and deployers of advanced AI technologies.

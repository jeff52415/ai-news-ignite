## 📰 Scientists use quantum machine learning to create semiconductors for the first time – and it could transform how chips are made

**Source:** Live Science  
**Date:** July 29, 2025  
**URL:** https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made
**Summary:** Australian scientists applied quantum machine learning (QML) to semiconductor manufacturing, introducing the Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR) that outperforms traditional machine learning models in modeling Ohmic contact resistance. This may revolutionize chip production.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Researchers at Australia's Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation (CSIRO) have developed a quantum machine learning technique called the Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR). This method accurately models Ohmic contact resistance in semiconductor devices, a critical factor affecting chip performance. The study, published in *Advanced Science*, demonstrates that QKAR outperforms traditional machine learning models in this application.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

Modeling Ohmic contact resistance is essential for optimizing semiconductor fabrication processes. Traditional machine learning approaches often struggle with this task due to the complex, nonlinear nature of the data. By leveraging quantum machine learning, QKAR can uncover intricate patterns in small, high-dimensional datasets, leading to more efficient and precise chip design. This advancement could significantly enhance the performance and reliability of future semiconductor devices.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **CSIRO (Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation):** Australia's national science agency, leading the research in quantum machine learning applications for semiconductor fabrication.

- **Professor Muhammad Usman:** Head of quantum systems research at CSIRO’s Data61, leading the study on quantum machine learning for semiconductor fabrication.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR):** A quantum machine learning model developed to accurately predict Ohmic contact resistance in semiconductor devices.

- **Data Utilized:** 159 experimental samples of gallium nitride high-electron-mobility transistors (GaN HEMTs).

- **Methodology:** QKAR encodes classical data into quantum states, allowing the quantum computer to analyze complex patterns. The results are then processed using classical machine learning algorithms to predict device performance.

---

### 📊 Benchmark Results

The study compared QKAR's performance against seven classical machine learning models, achieving superior results in multiple evaluation metrics. The mean absolute error (MAE) achieved by QKAR was 0.338 816;mm, demonstrating its effectiveness in modeling Ohmic contact resistance.

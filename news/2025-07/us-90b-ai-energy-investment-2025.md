**Summary:** President Trump announced up to $90 billion in private investments for AI and energy infrastructure in Pennsylvania, including major commitments from Blackstone, Google, CoreWeave, and Constellation Energy to boost data centers and clean energy supporting AI operations.

### What Happened

At the Pennsylvania Energy and Innovation Summit held at Carnegie Mellon University, President Donald Trump unveiled over $90 billion in private-sector investments aimed at enhancing artificial intelligence (AI) and energy infrastructure in Pennsylvania. This initiative includes substantial commitments from leading companies such as Blackstone, Google, CoreWeave, and Constellation Energy. The investments focus on developing data centers and clean energy solutions to support AI operations.

### Why It Matters

This significant investment underscores the United States' strategic emphasis on bolstering its position in the global AI race, particularly in competition with China. By enhancing AI capabilities and energy infrastructure, the U.S. aims to foster economic growth, job creation, and technological leadership. The initiative also addresses the escalating power demands associated with AI and cloud computing, ensuring a reliable and sustainable energy supply.

### Who's Involved

- **President Donald Trump**: Announced the investment initiative at the summit.
- **Blackstone**: Committed $25 billion to develop data centers and natural gas power plants in northeastern Pennsylvania. 
- **Google**: Pledged $25 billion for data center and AI infrastructure, including a $3 billion agreement to purchase hydroelectric power from Brookfield's facilities. 
- **CoreWeave**: Announced a $6 billion investment to establish a 300-megawatt AI data center in Lancaster, Pennsylvania. 
- **Constellation Energy**: Committed $2.4 billion to upgrade the Limerick nuclear power plant, aiming to add 340 megawatts of power capacity. 

### Technical Details

- **Blackstone's Investment**: Over $25 billion to develop data centers and natural gas power plants in northeastern Pennsylvania, with a joint venture with PPL Corporation for power generation. 
- **Google's Commitment**: $25 billion for data center and AI infrastructure, including a $3 billion agreement to purchase hydroelectric power from Brookfield's Holtwood and Safe Harbor facilities. 
- **CoreWeave's Data Center**: $6 billion investment to develop a 300-megawatt AI data center in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, with an initial 100-megawatt capacity and potential expansion to 300 megawatts. 
- **Constellation Energy's Upgrade**: $2.4 billion investment to uprate the Limerick nuclear power plant, adding 340 megawatts of power capacity.

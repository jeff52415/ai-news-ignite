## U.S. AI Action Plan Aims to Maintain Global Leadership

**Source:** AI Forum UK  
**Date:** 2025-07-28  
**URL:** [https://aiforum.org.uk/ai-news-roundup-july-28-2025/?utm_source=openai](https://aiforum.org.uk/ai-news-roundup-july-28-2025/?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** The White House unveiled an AI Action Plan with over 90 policies to drive U.S. innovation and leadership in AI over the next decade.

---

### What Happened

On July 23, 2025, the White House released "Winning the AI Race: America's AI Action Plan," a comprehensive strategy comprising over 90 federal policy actions aimed at reinforcing the United States' position as a global leader in artificial intelligence (AI). 

### Why It Matters

This initiative underscores the administration's commitment to fostering AI innovation, building robust infrastructure, and asserting leadership in international AI diplomacy and security. By implementing these policies, the U.S. aims to maintain its competitive edge in the rapidly evolving AI landscape, ensuring economic growth and national security.

### Who's Involved

- **President Donald J. Trump**: Announced the AI Action Plan, emphasizing its role in securing America's technological future.
- **Michael Kratsios**: Director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy, highlighted the plan's potential to drive innovation and global influence.
- **Marco Rubio**: Secretary of State and Acting National Security Advisor, emphasized the strategic importance of AI in national security.

### Technical Details

- **Policy Actions**: The plan identifies over 90 federal policy actions across three pillars:
  - **Accelerating Innovation**: Removing regulatory barriers to AI development and deployment.
  - **Building American AI Infrastructure**: Expediting permits for data centers and semiconductor fabrication facilities.
  - **Leading in International Diplomacy and Security**: Exporting American AI technology stacks to allies and partners.

- **Regulatory Changes**: The plan includes a government-wide review to repeal regulations hindering AI development, aiming to create a pro-innovation environment. 

- **Infrastructure Development**: Efforts to modernize data centers and semiconductor fabs are prioritized to support AI advancements. 

- **International Strategy**: The plan seeks to export the full AI technology stack—including hardware, models, software, applications, and standards—to global allies, countering adversarial influences.

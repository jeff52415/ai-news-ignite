---

### 🔹 What Happened

Ali Farhadi, CEO of the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), has called for greater openness in AI research. He contrasts Ai2's approach with the closed practices of many AI developers, emphasizing the importance of transparency in all components, including training data, to foster innovation and understanding. Farhadi highlights AI's transformative potential in scientific discovery and advocates for open-source AI models that allow complete access to all components. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/b20122deab3924ac4a6cf82d97327fae?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The call for openness in AI research is significant because it challenges the prevailing trend of secrecy among leading AI developers. By advocating for transparency, Farhadi aims to enable others to understand, modify, and build upon existing work, thereby accelerating innovation and ensuring that AI technologies are developed responsibly and inclusively.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Ali Farhadi**: CEO of the Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), advocating for open-source AI and transparency in research practices.
- **Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)**: A nonprofit research organization founded by Paul Allen, focusing on advancing AI for humanity's benefit.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Tulu 3 405B**: An AI model developed by Ai2 that rivals models from OpenAI and China's DeepSeek on several benchmarks.
- **Open-Source AI Models**: Models that provide complete access to all components, including training data, to enable understanding, modification, and further development.

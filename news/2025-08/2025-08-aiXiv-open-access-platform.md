# 📰 aiXiv: An AI-Generated and Open Access Platform for Human and AI Scientists

**Source:** arXiv  
**Date:** 2025-08-29  
**URL:** [https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15126?utm_source=openai](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15126?utm_source=openai)

## Summary

The aiXiv platform presents a pioneering multi-agent system where research proposals and papers are collaboratively submitted, reviewed, and iterated upon by both AI and human scientists. This initiative aims to significantly speed up the dissemination of high-quality AI-generated scientific research, fostering an innovative synergy between artificial and human intelligence in the scientific community.

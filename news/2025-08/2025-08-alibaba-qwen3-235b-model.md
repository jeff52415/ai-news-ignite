## 📰 Alibaba Retires Hybrid Mode, Introduces Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507 Model

**Source:**  ([actuia.com](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/alibaba-launches-qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-and-breaks-away-from-hybrid-reasoning/?utm_source=openai))  
**Date:** 2025-08-05  
**URL:** [https://www.alibabacloud.com/](https://www.alibabacloud.com/)  
**Summary:** Alibaba announces the retirement of its hybrid AI mode and rolls out Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507, a new large-scale AI model representing significant scale and performance improvements.

---

### What Happened
On July 21, 2025, Alibaba unveiled Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507, a large-scale AI model with 235 billion parameters. This model signifies a departure from Alibaba's previous hybrid reasoning approach, opting instead for separate training of Instruct and Thinking models to enhance quality.  ([actuia.com](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/alibaba-launches-qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-and-breaks-away-from-hybrid-reasoning/?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters
The introduction of Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507 marks a strategic shift in Alibaba's AI development, focusing on specialized model training to achieve superior performance. This move positions Alibaba as a formidable competitor in the AI landscape, challenging established models from companies like OpenAI and Anthropic.  ([actuia.com](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/alibaba-launches-qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-and-breaks-away-from-hybrid-reasoning/?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved
- **Alibaba Cloud**: The cloud computing arm of Alibaba Group, responsible for the development and deployment of Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507.

### Technical Details
- **Model Architecture**: Qwen3-235B-A22B-2507 utilizes a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with 128 specialized experts, activating 22 billion parameters per request.
- **Training Data**: The model was trained on a dataset comprising 36 trillion tokens, including textbooks, question-answer pairs, code snippets, and AI-generated content.  ([actuia.com](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/alibaba-launches-qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-and-breaks-away-from-hybrid-reasoning/?utm_source=openai))
- **Performance Benchmarks**:
  - **MMLU-Pro**: Achieved a score of 83.0, up from 75.2 in the previous version.
  - **MMLU-Redux**: Scored 93.1, approaching the level of Claude Opus 4 (94.2).
  - **AIME 2025**: Scored 70.3, surpassing DeepSeek-V3-0324 (46.6) and OpenAI's GPT-4o-0327 (26.7).
  - **MultiPL-E**: Scored 87.9, positioning it behind Claude (88.5) but ahead of GPT-4o and DeepSeek.
  - **LiveCodeBench v6**: Achieved a score of 51.8, marking the best performance measured on this benchmark.  ([actuia.com](https://www.actuia.com/en/news/alibaba-launches-qwen3-235b-a22b-instruct-2507-and-breaks-away-from-hybrid-reasoning/?utm_source=openai))

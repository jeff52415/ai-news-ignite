## 📰 Anthropic Unveils Claude 4: Autonomous AI for Complex Tasks

**Source:** Anthropic  
**Date:** 2025-08-08  
**URL:** [https://www.anthropic.com/news](https://www.anthropic.com/news)  
**Summary:** Anthropic has released Claude 4 with two key models, Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4, which can operate autonomously over long durations, improving performance on complex AI tasks.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Anthropic introduced Claude 4, a new generation of AI models designed for complex tasks. The release includes two models: Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4. These models are capable of operating autonomously over extended periods, enhancing their performance on intricate AI tasks.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The ability to perform complex tasks autonomously over long durations is a significant advancement in AI development. It allows for more efficient and effective handling of intricate problems, potentially transforming various industries by automating complex processes.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

Anthropic, an AI research company, is the developer behind Claude 4. The company has been at the forefront of AI safety and development, focusing on creating AI systems that align with human values.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Models:** Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4  
- **Capabilities:** Autonomous operation over extended periods  
- **Applications:** Complex AI tasks requiring sustained performance

### 🔹 Benchmark Results

While specific benchmark results for Claude 4 are not provided in the available sources, previous iterations have demonstrated significant improvements in autonomous operation and task performance.

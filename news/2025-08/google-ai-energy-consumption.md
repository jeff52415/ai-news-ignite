## 📰 Google's Transparency on AI Energy Consumption

**Source:** [Google's Approach to Energy Innovation and AI’s Environmental Footprint](https://blog.google/outreach-initiatives/sustainability/google-ai-energy-efficiency/)

**Date:** August 21, 2025

**Summary:** Google has disclosed detailed data on the energy use, water consumption, and carbon emissions of its AI assistant, Gemini Apps. A typical text prompt consumes energy equivalent to less than nine seconds of TV viewing and emits 0.03 grams of CO₂. Improvements in software efficiency and the use of clean energy have significantly reduced the environmental impact over the past year. However, this report excludes energy consumption from AI model training and multimedia tasks.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Google released a comprehensive methodology detailing the environmental footprint of its Gemini Apps AI assistant. The report highlights the energy consumption, water usage, and carbon emissions associated with processing text prompts. A single text prompt reportedly consumes 0.24 watt-hours of energy, equivalent to watching less than nine seconds of television, and emits 0.03 grams of CO₂. Over the past year, enhancements in software efficiency and the adoption of clean energy sources have led to a 33-fold reduction in energy consumption and a 44-fold decrease in carbon emissions per prompt. It’s important to note that this analysis does not account for the energy used during AI model training or the processing of multimedia prompts.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

As AI technologies become increasingly integrated into daily life, understanding and mitigating their environmental impact is crucial. Google’s transparency sets a precedent for the tech industry, encouraging other companies to disclose their environmental footprints and adopt sustainable practices. By sharing these findings, Google aims to foster industry-wide consistency in measuring and reducing AI's environmental effects.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Google:** The company responsible for developing the Gemini Apps AI assistant and conducting the environmental impact study.
- **Partha Ranganathan:** Google’s Vice President and Engineering Fellow, who emphasized the importance of environmental responsibility in AI development.
- **Savannah Goodman:** Google’s Head of Advanced Energy Labs, who highlighted the need for transparency in AI's environmental impact.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Gemini Apps AI Assistant:** Google's AI assistant whose environmental footprint was analyzed.
- **Energy Consumption:** 0.24 watt-hours per text prompt, equivalent to less than nine seconds of television viewing.
- **Carbon Emissions:** 0.03 grams of CO₂ per text prompt.
- **Efficiency Improvements:** 33-fold reduction in energy consumption and 44-fold decrease in carbon emissions per prompt over the past year.
- **Exclusions:** The study does not include energy used during AI model training or processing of multimedia prompts.

## Latent Labs Launches Web-Based AI Tool for Protein Design

**Source:** Latent Labs  
**Date:** 2025-08-04  
**URL:** [https://www.latentlabs.com/](https://www.latentlabs.com/)  
**Summary:** Latent Labs has introduced Latent-X, a web-based AI platform designed to democratize advanced protein design, providing accessible biotechnology research tools to a broader audience.

---

### What Happened

Latent Labs unveiled Latent-X, a generative AI model for protein binder design, accessible through a no-code, web-based platform. This tool enables users to design lab-ready macrocycles and mini-binders at all-atom resolution, streamlining the drug discovery process. Early access sign-ups are available at [platform.latentlabs.com](https://platform.latentlabs.com).

### Why It Matters

Traditional drug discovery methods are time-consuming and costly, often involving screening millions of molecules with low success rates. Latent-X offers a more efficient approach, allowing researchers to generate high-confidence binders with just 30	6 designs per target, significantly accelerating the development of new therapeutics.

### Who's Involved

- **Latent Labs**: The company behind Latent-X, aiming to revolutionize drug discovery through AI.  
- **Simon Kohl**: CEO and founder of Latent Labs, previously co-led DeepMinds AlphaFold2 protein prediction program. ([ft.com](https://www.ft.com/content/92143d49-c777-4bba-8857-b4ef7e82ebd4?utm_source=openai))

### Technical Details

- **Latent-X Model**: A generative AI model capable of designing functional, high-affinity protein binders at atomic resolution.  
- **Platform Features**:
  - No-code interface for designing protein binders.
  - Structure visualization and predicted structure overlays.
  - Computational ranking of designs for lab testing.
- **Validation Results**:
  - 91	6100% hit rates for macrocycles across three targets.
  - 10	664% hit rates for mini-binders across five targets.
  - Picomolar binding affinities for mini-binders and single-digit micromolar affinities for macrocycles.

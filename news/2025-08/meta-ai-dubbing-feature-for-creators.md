# Meta's AI Dubbing Feature for Creators

**Source:** ts2.tech

## Summary
Meta has introduced an AI-powered voice translation feature on Facebook and Instagram, enabling creators to automatically dub their videos into other languages while preserving their unique voice, tone, and style. The feature currently supports translations between English and Spanish, with plans to expand to additional languages in the future.

### What Happened
Meta has launched an AI-powered voice translation tool for creators on Facebook and Instagram. This feature allows creators to automatically dub their videos into another language, maintaining their original voice, tone, and style. An optional lip-sync feature aligns the dubbed audio with the creator's lip movements for a more authentic viewing experience. Initially, the tool supports translations between English and Spanish, with plans to add more languages over time. The feature is available to all public Instagram accounts and Facebook creators with at least 1,000 followers in regions where Meta AI is offered.

### Why It Matters
This development is significant as it enables creators to reach a broader, global audience by overcoming language barriers. By preserving the creator's unique voice and style, the feature ensures that the content remains authentic and engaging, regardless of the viewer's language. The addition of lip-syncing further enhances the realism of the dubbed videos, improving viewer experience. As the feature expands to support more languages, it has the potential to revolutionize content creation and consumption on Meta's platforms.

### Who's Involved
- Meta Platforms, Inc.: The company behind Facebook and Instagram, responsible for developing and deploying the AI-powered voice translation feature.
- Content Creators: Individuals and organizations using Facebook and Instagram to share content, who can now utilize this feature to expand their reach.

### Technical Details
- AI-Powered Voice Translation: Utilizes advanced artificial intelligence algorithms to translate and dub videos while preserving the creator's original voice, tone, and style.
- Lip-Sync Feature: An optional feature that aligns the dubbed audio with the creator's lip movements for a more authentic viewing experience.
- Language Support: Currently supports translations between English and Spanish, with plans to add more languages in the future.
- Availability: Accessible to all public Instagram accounts and Facebook creators with at least 1,000 followers in regions where Meta AI is offered.

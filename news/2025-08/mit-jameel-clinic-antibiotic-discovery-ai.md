## 📰 MIT Jameel Clinic Discovers Antibiotic Using AI

**Source:** MIT Jameel Clinic  
**Date:** May 25, 2023  
**URL:** [https://jclinic.mit.edu/research-project/deep-learning-guided-discovery-of-an-antibiotic-targeting-acinetobacter-baumannii/](https://jclinic.mit.edu/research-project/deep-learning-guided-discovery-of-an-antibiotic-targeting-acinetobacter-baumannii/)  
**Summary:** The MIT Jameel Clinic, in collaboration with McMaster University, utilized deep learning to discover abaucin, an antibiotic effective against the drug-resistant pathogen Acinetobacter baumannii.

---

### What Happened  
Researchers at the MIT Jameel Clinic and McMaster University employed machine learning techniques to identify abaucin, a novel antibiotic targeting Acinetobacter baumannii, a Gram-negative bacterium known for its multidrug resistance. This discovery was reported in the journal *Nature Chemical Biology* on May 25, 2023.

### Why It Matters  
The emergence of antibiotic-resistant bacteria poses a significant global health threat. Traditional methods of antibiotic discovery are often time-consuming and costly. By leveraging artificial intelligence, researchers can expedite the identification of new antibiotics, offering hope in the fight against resistant pathogens.

### Technical Details  
- **Model Used**: Deep learning-based neural network trained to predict antibacterial activity.  
- **Data**: Screened approximately 7,500 molecules for growth inhibition against A. baumannii.  
- **Discovery**: Identified abaucin, a narrow-spectrum antibiotic effective against A. baumannii.  
- **Mechanism**: Disrupts lipoprotein trafficking via LolE, a protein responsible for transferring lipoproteins from the inner to the outer membrane.  
- **In Vivo Validation**: Demonstrated efficacy in controlling A. baumannii infection in a mouse wound model.

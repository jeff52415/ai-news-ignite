##  Pika Labs Launches AI-Only Social Video App

**Source:** Pika Labs  
**Date:** 2025-08-06  
**URL:** [https://www.pikalabs.com/](https://www.pikalabs.com/)  
**Summary:** Pika Labs has introduced an AI-only social video app that enables users to create and share videos using advanced AI tools, enhancing creativity and social engagement.

---

###  What Happened
Pika Labs unveiled the first-ever AI-only social video app, allowing users to generate and share videos by leveraging advanced AI technologies. The app is currently in early access beta, with users able to join the waitlist or use an invite code to gain access. 

###  Why It Matters
This launch signifies a significant advancement in the integration of AI into social media platforms, offering users innovative tools to enhance their content creation and sharing experiences. By focusing on AI-driven video generation, Pika Labs aims to redefine how users interact with and produce content on social media. 

###  Who's Involved
- **Pika Labs:** The company behind the development and launch of the AI-only social video app.
- **Mellis, Inc.:** The developer of the Pika app, offering AI-powered video creation tools. 

###  Technical Details
- **Platform:** iOS (currently in early access beta).
- **Features:**
  - **Selfie-Based Video Generation:** Users can create videos by transforming selfies into dynamic, AI-generated content.
  - **Audio-Driven Video Generation:** A new AI model enables the creation of videos with native audio generation, allowing for videos of any length in HD quality. ([gadgets360.com](https://www.gadgets360.com/ai/news/pika-labs-social-ai-app-ios-released-new-audio-enabled-video-generation-model-unveiled-9068602/amp?utm_source=openai))
  - **Pikaffects:** A feature that allows users to apply various effects to their videos, such as AI Dance, Transition, Festival, Squish, Muscle Surge, Fight Club, Anything Robot, and Long Hair Magic.

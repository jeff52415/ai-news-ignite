## 📰 Quantum Machine Learning Revolutionizes Semiconductor Manufacturing

**Source:** LiveScience  
**Date:** August 15, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made](https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made)  
**Summary:** Australian scientists introduced Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR) using quantum machine learning to model Ohmic contact resistance, outperforming classical AI and potentially transforming chip manufacturing.

---

### What Happened

Australian researchers have developed a quantum machine learning technique called Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR) to model Ohmic contact resistance in semiconductor chips. This method outperforms traditional machine learning models, potentially revolutionizing chip manufacturing processes. ([livescience.com](https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters

Accurately modeling Ohmic contact resistance is crucial for optimizing chip performance. QKAR's superior accuracy over classical AI models could lead to more efficient and precise semiconductor fabrication, impacting various electronic devices. ([livescience.com](https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved

- **Researchers:** A team from Australia developed QKAR, combining quantum computing and machine learning to enhance semiconductor manufacturing. ([livescience.com](https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made?utm_source=openai))

### Technical Details

- **Model:** Quantum Kernel-Aligned Regressor (QKAR)
- **Method:** Converts classical data into quantum states, analyzes patterns using quantum computing, and applies machine learning for prediction.
- **Data:** 159 samples of gallium nitride high-electron-mobility transistors (GaN HEMTs)
- **Benchmark Results:** QKAR outperformed seven classical AI models, indicating its superior ability to handle small, high-dimensional datasets. ([livescience.com](https://www.livescience.com/technology/computing/scientists-use-quantum-machine-learning-to-create-semiconductors-for-the-first-time-and-it-could-transform-how-chips-are-made?utm_source=openai))

## Summary

A Singapore-based AI company developed the Hierarchical Reasoning Model (HRM), inspired by human brain processing. Despite having only 27 million parameters and training on just 1,000 samples, HRM outperforms leading large language models like ChatGPT on complex reasoning benchmarks. This breakthrough suggests a promising path towards artificial general intelligence (AGI), challenging the convention that bigger models trained on massive datasets are necessary for advanced reasoning.

The HRM's success highlights the importance of hierarchical and brain-inspired approaches in AI development, potentially reshaping future AI research and applications.

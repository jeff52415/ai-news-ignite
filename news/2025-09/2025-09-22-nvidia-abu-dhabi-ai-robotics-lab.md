## Nvidia and Abu Dhabi Institute Launch Joint AI and Robotics Lab

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** 2025-09-22  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/nvidia-abu-dhabi-institute-launch-joint-ai-robotics-lab-uae-2025-09-22/](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/nvidia-abu-dhabi-institute-launch-joint-ai-robotics-lab-uae-2025-09-22/)  
**Summary:** Nvidia and Abu Dhabi's Technology Innovation Institute (TII) have collaborated to establish a joint AI and robotics research lab in the UAE, marking the first Nvidia AI Technology Center in the Middle East. The lab aims to develop advanced robotics using Nvidia's new Thor chip.

---

### What Happened

Nvidia and Abu Dhabi's Technology Innovation Institute (TII) have launched a joint AI and robotics research lab in the UAE. This collaboration combines TII's multidisciplinary research capabilities with Nvidia’s advanced AI models and computing power. The initiative aims to develop cutting-edge AI technologies and robotics, including humanoids, robotic arms, and four-legged robots. A key component will be the deployment of Nvidia’s new Thor chip, designed for advanced robotic systems. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/nvidia-abu-dhabi-institute-launch-joint-ai-robotics-lab-uae-2025-09-22/?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters

This partnership signifies a strategic move to position the UAE as a global leader in AI and robotics. By integrating Nvidia’s advanced computing platforms with TII’s research expertise, the lab is set to accelerate the development of intelligent systems capable of operating in complex, real-world environments. The use of the Thor chip is expected to enhance the capabilities of robotic systems, enabling them to reason, adapt, and act autonomously. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/nvidia-abu-dhabi-institute-launch-joint-ai-robotics-lab-uae-2025-09-22/?utm_source=openai))

### Who’s Involved

- **Nvidia:** A leading company in accelerated computing and AI, providing advanced AI models and computing power.
- **Technology Innovation Institute (TII):** The applied research arm of Abu Dhabi’s Advanced Technology Research Council, focusing on AI, robotics, and high-performance computing.

### Technical Details

- **Thor Chip:** Nvidia’s new edge GPU designed to enable advanced robotic systems development. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/nvidia-abu-dhabi-institute-launch-joint-ai-robotics-lab-uae-2025-09-22/?utm_source=openai))
- **Research Focus:** Development of humanoid robots, four-legged robots, and robotic arms.
- **Integration:** Combining TII’s research in AI, robotics, autonomous systems, and high-performance computing with Nvidia’s AI models and computing platforms.

## AI Models Solve Complex Mathematical Problems

**Source:** Axios  
**Date:** September 12, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-science-09a179e0-486c-11ef-9d2e-139469c63b1d](https://www.axios.com/newsletters/axios-science-09a179e0-486c-11ef-9d2e-139469c63b1d) (link currently inaccessible)  
**Summary:** Google DeepMind's AI systems AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 achieved silver medals by solving complex problems in the International Mathematical Olympiad, showcasing AI's growing math proficiency.

---

### What Happened

Google DeepMind's AI systems, AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2, participated in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) and earned silver medals by solving complex mathematical problems. This achievement highlights the advancing capabilities of AI in mathematical reasoning.

### Why It Matters

The success of AI models in the IMO signifies a substantial advancement in artificial intelligence's ability to perform complex mathematical reasoning, potentially transforming fields that rely on mathematical problem-solving.

### Who's Involved

- **Google DeepMind:** Developed the AI systems AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2.
- **International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO):** The organization that conducts the annual competition.

### Technical Details

- **AlphaProof:** A reinforcement-learning-based system designed for formal mathematical reasoning.
- **AlphaGeometry 2:** An enhanced version of DeepMind's geometry-solving system, capable of addressing complex geometric problems.

---

### Benchmark Results

The AI systems achieved a score of 28 out of 42 points in the IMO, earning a silver medal. The gold medal threshold was 29 points, achieved by 58 of 609 contestants.

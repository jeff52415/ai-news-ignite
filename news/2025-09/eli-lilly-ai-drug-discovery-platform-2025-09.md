##  Eli Lilly Launches AI-Enabled Drug Discovery Platform

**Source:** Eli Lilly Official Release  
**Date:** September 9, 2025   
**URL:** [https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies)  
**Summary:** Eli Lilly has introduced TuneLab, an AI and machine learning platform designed to democratize access to advanced drug discovery models and accelerate new therapy developments in biotech.

---

###  What Happened

Eli Lilly and Company announced the launch of TuneLab, an artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) platform that provides biotechnology companies access to drug discovery models trained on years of Lilly's research data. This initiative aims to democratize access to powerful AI tools, enabling smaller biotech firms to leverage advanced capabilities previously limited to large pharmaceutical companies.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))

###  Why It Matters

The introduction of TuneLab signifies a strategic move by Eli Lilly to foster innovation and collaboration within the biotech ecosystem. By sharing its extensive datasets, valued at over $1 billion, Lilly aims to accelerate the development of new medicines and therapies, particularly in areas where smaller companies may lack the resources to build similar AI models.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))

###  Who's Involved

- **Eli Lilly and Company**: The pharmaceutical giant behind the development and launch of TuneLab.
- **Daniel Skovronsky, M.D., Ph.D.**: Chief Scientific Officer and President of Lilly Research Laboratories and Lilly Immunology, who emphasized the platform's role in leveling the playing field for smaller biotech companies.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))
- **Circle Pharma and insitro**: Privately held biotech companies partnering with Lilly to utilize TuneLab for developing cancer therapies and small molecule drugs, respectively.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))

###  Technical Details

- **Platform Name**: TuneLab  
- **Technology**: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning  
- **Data Utilized**: Proprietary datasets valued at over $1 billion, including drug disposition, safety, and preclinical data from hundreds of thousands of unique molecules.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))  
- **Data Sharing Model**: Federated learning, allowing biotech companies to access Lilly's AI models without directly exposing their proprietary data.  ([Eli Lilly Press Release](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies))

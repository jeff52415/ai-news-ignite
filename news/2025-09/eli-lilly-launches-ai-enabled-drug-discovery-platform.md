##  Eli Lilly Launches AI-Enabled Drug Discovery Platform

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** September 9, 2025  
**URL:** [https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies](https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lilly-launches-tunelab-platform-give-biotechnology-companies)  
**Summary:** Eli Lilly introduced TuneLab, an AI/ML drug discovery platform to democratize access to advanced model deployment, accelerating therapy development for smaller biotech firms.

---

###  What Happened

Eli Lilly and Company announced the launch of TuneLab, an artificial intelligence and machine learning (AI/ML) platform designed to provide biotechnology companies with access to drug discovery models trained on years of Lilly's proprietary research data. This initiative aims to democratize access to advanced AI capabilities, enabling smaller biotech firms to accelerate the development of new therapies.

###  Why It Matters

The introduction of TuneLab is significant because it levels the playing field in drug discovery by granting smaller biotech companies access to sophisticated AI models that were previously exclusive to large pharmaceutical corporations. This democratization can lead to faster and more cost-effective development of new medicines, potentially addressing unmet medical needs more efficiently.

###  Who's Involved

- **Eli Lilly and Company**: The pharmaceutical giant behind the development and launch of TuneLab.
- **Circle Pharma**: A privately held biotech company partnering with Lilly to develop cancer therapies using TuneLab.
- **Insitro**: Another biotech firm collaborating with Lilly to build new AI models for the discovery of small molecule therapies.

###  Technical Details

- **Platform Name**: TuneLab
- **Technology**: Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML)
- **Data Utilized**: Proprietary datasets valued at over $1 billion, including preclinical, safety, and drug disposition data from experiments with hundreds of thousands of unique molecules.
- **Methodology**: Federated learning, a privacy-preserving approach that allows biotech companies to access Lilly's AI models without directly exposing their proprietary data or Lilly's.

##  Google Expands AI-Powered Search to Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Brazilian Portuguese

**Source:** Champaign Magazine  
**Date:** September 14, 2025  
**URL:** [https://champaignmagazine.com/2025/09/14/ai-by-ai-weekly-top-5-september-8-14-2025/?utm_source=openai](https://champaignmagazine.com/2025/09/14/ai-by-ai-weekly-top-5-september-8-14-2025/?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** Google has extended its AI-powered search interface, known as AI Mode, to five new languagesHindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Brazilian Portuguese enhancing accessibility for non-English speakers and reinforcing its commitment to global inclusivity in AI search tools.

---

###  What Happened

Google announced the expansion of its AI Mode in Search to five new languages: Hindi, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, and Brazilian Portuguese. This move aims to make AI-powered search capabilities more accessible to users worldwide. 【source】([Google Blog](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-expands-more-languages/?utm_source=openai))

###  Why It Matters

By broadening language support, Google is enhancing the inclusivity and relevance of its AI search tools, ensuring that users can interact with search results in their native languages. This expansion reflects Google's commitment to providing culturally and contextually appropriate information to a global audience. 【source】([Google Blog](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-expands-more-languages/?utm_source=openai))

###  Who's Involved

- **Google:** The company behind the development and deployment of AI Mode in Search.
- **Hema Budaraju:** Vice President of Product Management at Google Search, who highlighted the importance of understanding local information for a truly global search experience. 【source】([Google Blog](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-expands-more-languages/?utm_source=openai))

###  Technical Details

- **AI Mode:** An AI-powered search experience that utilizes advanced multimodal and reasoning capabilities.
- **Gemini 2.5:** A customized version of Google's AI model integrated into AI Mode to enhance language understanding and provide locally relevant search capabilities. 【source】([Google Blog](https://blog.google/products/search/ai-mode-expands-more-languages/?utm_source=openai))

## Meta Unveils New AI Video Feed 'Vibes'

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** September 25, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-unveils-new-ai-video-feed-vibes-2025-09-25](https://www.reuters.com/business/meta-unveils-new-ai-video-feed-vibes-2025-09-25) (link currently unavailable)  
**Summary:** Meta has launched 'Vibes,' an AI-powered video feed that enables users to create and share short AI-generated videos by remixing existing content or starting from scratch. These videos can be shared on Vibes, Instagram, and Facebook stories and reels via the Meta AI app.

---

### What Happened

Meta Platforms introduced 'Vibes,' a new AI-driven video feed integrated into the Meta AI app and accessible via meta.ai. This platform allows users to create and share short-form, AI-generated videos by starting from scratch, using existing content, or remixing videos from the feed. Users can add new visuals, layer in music, and adjust styles to personalize their creations. The videos can be shared directly on Vibes or cross-posted to Instagram and Facebook stories and reels.

### Why It Matters

The launch of Vibes signifies Meta's strategic move to accelerate its artificial intelligence initiatives. By providing users with tools to create and share AI-generated content, Meta aims to enhance user engagement and expand its presence in the AI-driven content creation space. This development follows Meta's reorganization of its AI efforts in June 2025 under the division 'Superintelligence Labs,' established after challenges with its open-source Llama 4 model and key staff departures.

### Who's Involved

- **Meta Platforms Inc.**: Developer and distributor of Vibes.
- **Alexandr Wang**: Meta's Chief AI Officer, who mentioned cooperation with AI image generators Midjourney and Black Forest Labs in creating Vibes.

### Technical Details

- **Platform:** Vibes is available through the Meta AI app and meta.ai.
- **Features:** Create videos from scratch, remix existing video content, add visuals and music layers, adjust styles.
- **Partnerships:** Collaboration with Midjourney and Black Forest Labs for AI-generated content in the early Vibes version.

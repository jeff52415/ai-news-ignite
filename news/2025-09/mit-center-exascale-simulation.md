## MIT Establishes Center for Exascale Simulation

**Source:** MIT News  
**Date:** September 10, 2025  
**URL:** https://news.mit.edu/2025/mit-center-exascale-simulation-coupled-high-enthalpy-fluid-solid-interactions-0910  
**Summary:** The U.S. Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration (DOE/NNSA) has selected MIT to establish the Center for the Exascale Simulation of Coupled High-Enthalpy Fluid–Solid Interactions (CHEFSI). This center aims to advance the simulation of extreme environments, such as those encountered in hypersonic flight and atmospheric re-entry, by leveraging exascale supercomputers and next-generation algorithms.

---

### What Happened

MIT has been chosen by the DOE/NNSA to lead a new research center focused on enhancing the predictive simulation of extreme environments. The center, named CHEFSI, will concentrate on high-enthalpy fluid–solid interactions, which are critical in scenarios like hypersonic flight and atmospheric re-entry. This initiative is part of the fourth phase of NNSA's Predictive Science Academic Alliance Program (PSAAP-IV), which supports frontier research to advance high-performance computing capabilities for applications relevant to national security.

### Why It Matters

Understanding and accurately simulating extreme environments is vital for the development of advanced thermal protection systems, reusable spacecraft, and hypersonic vehicles. By establishing CHEFSI, MIT aims to provide critical insights into material performance and failure under high-enthalpy conditions, thereby contributing to advancements in national security and space exploration.

### Who's Involved

- **Raúl Radovitzky**: Jerome C. Hunsaker Professor of Aeronautics and Astronautics, associate director of the Institute for Soldier Nanotechnologies (ISN), and director of CHEFSI.

- **Youssef Marzouk**: Breene M. Kerr (1951) Professor of Aeronautics and Astronautics, co-director of the MIT Center for Computational Science and Engineering (CCSE), and associate dean of the MIT Schwarzman College of Computing.

- **Nicolas Hadjiconstantinou**: Quentin Berg (1937) Professor of Mechanical Engineering and co-director of CCSE.

- **Franklin Hadley**: Leads center operations.

- **Joshua Freedman**: Oversees administration and finance.

The center will also collaborate with DOE/NNSA national laboratories, including Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Los Alamos National Laboratory, and Sandia National Laboratories.

### Technical Details

- **Models and Methods**: CHEFSI will integrate high-fidelity physics models with artificial intelligence-based surrogate models and state-of-the-art exascale computational tools. This approach aims to simulate high-enthalpy gas flows and the chemical, thermal, and mechanical behavior of solid materials, capturing phenomena such as oxidation, nitridation, ablation, and fracture.

- **Data**: The center will utilize exascale supercomputers and next-generation algorithms to perform detailed simulations of extreme environments characterized by temperatures exceeding 1,500 degrees Celsius and speeds up to Mach 25.

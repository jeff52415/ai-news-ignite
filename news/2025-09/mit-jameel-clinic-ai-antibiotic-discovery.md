## 📰 MIT Jameel Clinic Uses AI to Discover New Antibiotic Targeting Drug-Resistant Infections

**Source:**  ([jclinic.mit.edu](https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/?utm_source=openai))  
**Date:** September 20, 2025  
**URL:** [https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/](https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/)  
**Summary:** MIT's Jameel Clinic utilized deep learning to discover abaucin, a novel antibiotic effective against the drug-resistant bacterium *Acinetobacter baumannii*.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Researchers at MIT's Jameel Clinic employed machine learning techniques to identify abaucin, a potent antibiotic targeting *Acinetobacter baumannii*, a bacterium known for its resistance to multiple drugs.  ([jclinic.mit.edu](https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The emergence of antibiotic-resistant bacteria poses a significant global health threat. The discovery of abaucin represents a promising advancement in combating infections caused by such resistant pathogens.  ([jclinic.mit.edu](https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Model**: A message-passing neural network trained to predict antibacterial activity against *A. baumannii*.
- **Data**: Screened approximately 7,500 compounds for growth inhibition of *A. baumannii*.
- **Discovery**: Identified abaucin, a narrow-spectrum antibiotic effective against *A. baumannii*.
- **Mechanism**: Disrupts lipoprotein trafficking via LolE, a protein responsible for transferring lipoproteins from the inner to the outer membrane.
- **In Vivo Validation**: Demonstrated efficacy in controlling *A. baumannii* infection in a mouse wound model.  ([jclinic.mit.edu](https://jclinic.mit.edu/antibiotic-identified-by-ai/?utm_source=openai))

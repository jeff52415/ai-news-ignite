## 📰 Sereact Raises €25M to Advance AI Robotics in Germany

**Source:**  
**Date:** January 20, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.timesofai.com/news/machine-learning/?utm_source=openai](https://www.timesofai.com/news/machine-learning/?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** German robotics company Sereact secured €25 million in Series A funding to develop AI-driven software for humanoid and mobile robotics platforms.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Sereact, a Stuttgart-based robotics startup, announced the successful closure of a €25 million Series A funding round. The investment was led by Creandum, with participation from existing investors Point Nine and Air Street Capital, and notable business angels including former Formula 1 World Champion Nico Rosberg, Mehdi Ghissassi (ex-Google DeepMind), Ott Kaukver (Skype), Lars Nordwall (ex-neo4j), Rubin Ritter (ex-Zalando), Torsten Reil, and Niklas Köhler (both from Helsing). 

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This funding is pivotal for Sereact to advance its AI-powered robotics solutions, particularly in the development of Vision Language Action Models (VLAMs). These models enable robots to understand and adapt to their environments in real-time without the need for extensive programming. The company's approach is hardware-agnostic, allowing their software to be integrated across various robotic platforms, including articulated arms, mobile robots, and humanoid systems. 

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Sereact:** A Stuttgart-based startup specializing in AI-driven robotics solutions.
- **Creandum:** Lead investor in the Series A funding round.
- **Point Nine and Air Street Capital:** Existing investors participating in the funding.
- **Business Angels:** Including Nico Rosberg, Mehdi Ghissassi, Ott Kaukver, Lars Nordwall, Rubin Ritter, Torsten Reil, and Niklas Köhler.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Vision Language Action Models (VLAMs):** Enable robots to perform zero-shot reasoning, allowing them to understand and act in environments without specific prior training.
- **Hardware-Agnostic Approach:** Sereact's software is designed to be compatible with various robotic hardware platforms, facilitating broader adoption and integration.

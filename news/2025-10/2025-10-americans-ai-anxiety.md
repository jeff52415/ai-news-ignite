##  Americans Lead the World in AI Anxiety

**Source:** Axios  
**Date:** October 15, 2025  
**URL:** https://www.axios.com/2025/10/15/ai-regulation-china-us-pew-report?utm_source=openai  
**Summary:** A Pew Research Center study reveals that half of Americans are more worried than excited about AI's growing presence in daily life, influencing future AI adoption.

---

###  What Happened

A recent Pew Research Center report indicates that Americans are the most anxious about the increasing presence of artificial intelligence (AI) in daily life. Half of U.S. respondents expressed more concern than excitement about AI developments, highlighting a national unease that may impact how the technology is adopted and integrated into workplaces.

###  Why It Matters

Public concern over AI could shape how quickly the tools are adopted and could affect workplaces if employees aren't comfortable with the changes. The report also highlights a global distrust in both the U.S. and China when it comes to regulating AI technology.

###  Who's Involved

- **Pew Research Center**: Conducted the study revealing public sentiment towards AI.
- **Axios**: Reported on the findings of the Pew Research Center study.

###  Technical Details

- **Study Methodology**: The Pew Research Center surveyed 28,333 adults across 25 countries, covering six regions.

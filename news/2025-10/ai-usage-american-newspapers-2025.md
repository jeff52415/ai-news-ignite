## 📰 AI Usage in American Newspapers: Widespread, Uneven, and Rarely Disclosed

**Source:** arXiv  
**Date:** 2025-10-25  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.18774  
**Summary:** A study of 186,000 articles reveals about 9% of new newspaper content is partially or fully AI-generated, unevenly distributed with more prevalence in smaller outlets and topics like weather, highlighting transparency issues.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

A recent study analyzed 186,000 articles from online editions of 1,500 American newspapers published in the summer of 2025. Using Pangram, a state-of-the-art AI detector, researchers found that approximately 9% of newly published articles were either partially or fully AI-generated. This AI usage was more prevalent in smaller, local outlets and specific topics such as weather and technology. Additionally, opinion pieces from major publications like the Washington Post, New York Times, and Wall Street Journal were 6.4 times more likely to contain AI-generated content compared to their news articles. Despite this prevalence, only 5 out of 100 AI-flagged articles disclosed the use of AI.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The widespread and undisclosed use of AI in journalism raises significant concerns about transparency and trust. As AI-generated content becomes more common, readers may unknowingly consume information produced by algorithms rather than human journalists, potentially affecting the quality and reliability of news. The lack of disclosure further complicates the issue, making it challenging for audiences to discern the origins of the content they encounter.

# AI Adoption Among Researchers Surges, But Expectations Tempered

**Source:** Phys.org  
**Date:** October 26, 2025  
**URL:** [https://phys.org/news/2025-10-ai-significant-reality.html](https://phys.org/news/2025-10-ai-significant-reality.html)

## Summary
Researchers' use of AI grew from 57% to 84% in a year, improving efficiency and work quality, but firsthand experiences are causing tempered expectations about AI capabilities.

---

### What Happened

A recent study by Wiley, titled "ExplanAItions," surveyed over 2,400 researchers worldwide and found that AI adoption among researchers increased from 57% in 2024 to 84% in 2025. The study also revealed that while researchers acknowledge AI's benefits in enhancing efficiency and work quality, their expectations have become more realistic based on firsthand experiences.

### Why It Matters

The surge in AI adoption signifies a significant shift in the research community's approach to technology, highlighting the growing integration of AI tools in academic and professional settings. However, the tempered expectations suggest a more cautious and informed perspective on AI's current capabilities and limitations.

### Who's Involved

- **Wiley:** Conducted the "ExplanAItions" study to assess AI adoption among researchers.
- **Jay Flynn:** Executive Vice President & General Manager, Research & Learning at Wiley, who commented on the study's findings.

### Technical Details

- **Study Methodology:** Surveyed over 2,400 researchers worldwide.
- **Key Findings:**
  - AI adoption among researchers increased from 57% in 2024 to 84% in 2025.
  - Researchers' expectations of AI's capabilities have become more realistic based on firsthand experiences.
  - Concerns about AI inaccuracies and privacy have risen, with 64% expressing concerns about inaccuracies and 58% about privacy and security.

## Concerns Over AI-Generated Papers in Academic Publishing

**Source:** Times Higher Education  
**Date:** October 16, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing)  
**Summary:** A global survey reveals that 53% of researchers are concerned that AI-generated content is overwhelming peer review processes, leading to low-quality research and integrity issues.

---

### What Happened

A global survey of over 3,000 researchers, conducted by Cambridge University Press, found that 53% believe AI is negatively impacting academic publishing. Respondents expressed concerns about AI-generated content overwhelming peer review processes and leading to low-quality research. ([timeshighereducation.com](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters

The rise of AI-generated papers poses significant challenges to the integrity and credibility of academic publishing. The influx of low-quality content can strain peer review systems, making it harder to assess meaningful research and potentially undermining the foundations of scientific rigor. ([timeshighereducation.com](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved

- **Cambridge University Press:** Conducted the survey revealing widespread concerns among researchers.
- **Mandy Hill:** Managing Director of Cambridge University Press, highlighted the risks of AI-generated content overwhelming important work. ([timeshighereducation.com](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing?utm_source=openai))

### Technical Details

- **Survey Methodology:** The "Publishing Futures" report is based on a survey of over 3,000 researchers worldwide.
- **Key Findings:**
  - 53% of respondents believe AI negatively impacts academic publishing.
  - 33% are concerned that AI makes it harder for researchers to gain due credit for their work.
  - 81% agree that the increase in research outputs has put the peer review system under pressure. ([timeshighereducation.com](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/news/low-quality-and-ai-generated-papers-could-overwhelm-publishing?utm_source=openai))

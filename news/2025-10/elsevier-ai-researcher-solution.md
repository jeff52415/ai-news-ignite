## 📰 Elsevier Announces Next-Generation AI-Powered Researcher Solution

**Source:** PR Newswire  
**Date:** 2025-10-22  
**URL:** [https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/redefining-research-elsevier-announces-next-generation-ai-powered-researcher-solution-302556240.html](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/redefining-research-elsevier-announces-next-generation-ai-powered-researcher-solution-302556240.html)  
**Summary:** Elsevier introduced a new AI-powered tool designed to help researchers quickly identify emerging topics, synthesize literature, and collaborate, aiming to accelerate scientific breakthroughs.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Elsevier announced the development of a next-generation, end-to-end AI-powered solution for academic and corporate researchers. This tool is designed to transform the research workflow by assisting scientists in moving faster from insights to impact while maintaining research integrity, transparency, and trust.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

The new AI solution aims to address common challenges faced by researchers, such as information overload and time-consuming literature reviews. By providing a comprehensive, AI-driven assistant, Elsevier seeks to enhance productivity, foster collaboration, and accelerate scientific discoveries.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Elsevier**: A global leader in advanced information and decision support, Elsevier is developing the AI-powered solution in collaboration with the research community.
- **Professor Minoru Terano**: President of the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST), he emphasized the importance of emerging AI tools in supporting original human thinking and creativity.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **AI Technologies**: The solution integrates agentic AI, generative AI, reasoning engines, and retrieval-augmented generation to provide comprehensive support for researchers.
- **Content Sources**: It leverages millions of peer-reviewed articles and book chapters from leading academic publishers, ensuring depth and coverage.
- **Features**:
  - **One Seamless Assistant**: Enables brainstorming, project planning, literature review, collaboration, and funding discovery within a single platform.
  - **Trust Cards**: Displays how evidence was used or inferred, highlighting confidence levels and providing risk assessments for potential inaccuracies.
  - **Certified Content**: Provides access to comprehensive, peer-reviewed, cross-publisher academic content.
  - **Curated Datasets**: Answers powered by publisher-neutral datasets, such as Scopus abstracts and funding data.
  - **Add Your Own Content**: Allows users to supplement existing content with their own materials.
  - **Privacy and Security**: Built with enterprise-grade security, developed in line with Elsevier's Privacy Principles to safeguard personal data and privacy.
  - **Publisher-Neutral Algorithms**: An independent Advisory Board ensures results are prioritized and ranked based on quality, in a transparent, unbiased, and responsible manner.

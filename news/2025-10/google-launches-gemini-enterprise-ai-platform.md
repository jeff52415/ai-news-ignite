## Google launches Gemini Enterprise AI platform for business clients

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** 2025-10-09  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/business/google-launches-gemini-enterprise-ai-platform-business-clients-2025-10-09/](https://www.reuters.com/business/google-launches-gemini-enterprise-ai-platform-business-clients-2025-10-09/)  
**Summary:** Google introduced Gemini Enterprise, an AI platform that enables businesses to interact conversationally with their data, documents, and applications. It features pre-built AI agents for research and data analysis and allows custom agent creation. Early adopters include Gap, Figma, and Klarna.

---

### What Happened

Google launched Gemini Enterprise, a new AI platform designed for business clients. This platform leverages Google's latest AI models to facilitate conversational interactions with organizational data, documents, and applications. It offers pre-built AI agents for tasks such as deep research and data analysis and provides tools for companies to create and deploy custom AI agents. Early adopters include Gap, Figma, and Klarna.

### Why It Matters

The introduction of Gemini Enterprise signifies Google's strategic move to expand its presence in the enterprise AI market, competing with established players like Microsoft, OpenAI, and Anthropic. By offering a platform that integrates advanced AI capabilities into business workflows, Google aims to enhance productivity and decision-making processes for organizations across various industries.

### Who's Involved

- **Google:** Developed and launched the Gemini Enterprise platform.
- **Gap:** Apparel retailer utilizing Gemini Enterprise to enhance trend detection and accelerate product development.
- **Figma:** Design software company adopting Gemini Enterprise for improved design workflows.
- **Klarna:** Buy-now-pay-later lender integrating Gemini Enterprise to streamline financial services.

### Technical Details

- **Platform:** Gemini Enterprise integrates Google's latest AI models, including Gemini 2.5 Flash Image (nicknamed "nano banana") and Gemini Code Assist.
- **Features:** Pre-built AI agents for research and data analysis, tools for creating custom AI agents, and integration capabilities with platforms like Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Salesforce, and SAP.
- **Pricing:** Gemini Enterprise is available globally through three pricing tiers: Standard and Plus ($30 per seat/month) for larger corporations with strong security needs, and Business ($21 per seat/month) for small businesses and startups.

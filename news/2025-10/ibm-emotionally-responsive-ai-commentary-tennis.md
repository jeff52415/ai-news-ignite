## 📰 IBM Pilots Emotionally Responsive AI Commentary for Tennis Events

**Source:** Crescendo.ai  
**Date:** 2025-10-18  
**URL:** [https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates?utm_source=openai](https://www.crescendo.ai/news/latest-ai-news-and-updates?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** IBM's new AI commentary system uses computer vision and speech models to dynamically adjust tone and enthusiasm during tennis matches, enhancing viewer experience.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

IBM has developed an AI-driven commentary system that analyzes tennis match footage in real-time to generate dynamic audio commentary. This system adjusts its tone and enthusiasm based on the match's context, aiming to provide a more immersive experience for viewers. The technology was piloted during recent tennis events, including the 2025 Wimbledon Championships and the 2025 US Open. 

### 🔹 Why It Matters

Traditional sports commentary relies heavily on human announcers, which can be resource-intensive and limited in scalability. IBM's AI commentary system offers a scalable solution that can provide consistent and timely commentary for a vast number of matches, including those without live human commentators. By dynamically adjusting its tone and enthusiasm, the AI aims to enhance viewer engagement and satisfaction.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **IBM:** Developed the AI commentary system using its watsonx platform, which integrates generative AI and data analytics.
- **The All England Lawn Tennis Club (AELTC):** Collaborated with IBM to implement the AI commentary system during the 2025 Wimbledon Championships.
- **United States Tennis Association (USTA):** Partnered with IBM to introduce the AI commentary system at the 2025 US Open.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Computer Vision Module:** Extracts play-by-play metadata by analyzing match footage, including court and net detection, player and ball tracking, player pose estimation, and shot classification.
- **Generative AI Models:** Utilize large language models fine-tuned on tennis-specific data to generate natural language commentary. These models adjust their tone and enthusiasm based on the match's context.
- **Integration with Digital Platforms:** The AI commentary is integrated into digital platforms like the Wimbledon App and USOpen.org, providing fans with real-time, on-demand commentary for match highlights.

### 📊 Benchmark Results

Specific benchmark results for the AI commentary system are not publicly disclosed. However, the system has been deployed successfully during major tennis events, indicating its effectiveness in real-world applications.

## 📰 India Proposes Strict Regulations for AI-Generated Content

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** 2025-10-22  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/india-proposes-strict-it-rules-labelling-deepfakes-amid-ai-misuse-2025-10-22/](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/india-proposes-strict-it-rules-labelling-deepfakes-amid-ai-misuse-2025-10-22/)  
**Summary:** India has proposed new regulations requiring AI and social media companies to clearly label AI-generated content to combat misinformation and deepfakes. The rules mandate that platforms label AI-generated visual media with a marker covering at least 10% of the image and audio clips with an identifier during the first 10% of playback. Users must also declare whether their content is AI-generated, and companies must implement technical systems for verification.

---

###  What Happened
India's government has proposed new regulations requiring AI and social media companies to clearly label AI-generated content to curb the spread of misinformation and deepfakes. The rules mandate that platforms label AI-generated visual media with a marker covering at least 10% of the image and audio clips with an identifier during the first 10% of playback. Additionally, users must declare whether their content is AI-generated, and companies must implement technical systems for verification.

###  Why It Matters
With nearly a billion internet users, India is particularly vulnerable to the spread of misinformation and deepfakes, which can manipulate elections, impersonate individuals, and incite conflict. The proposed regulations aim to increase transparency and trust in AI applications by clearly identifying content created using artificial intelligence.

###  Who's Involved
- **Indian Government:** Proposing the new regulations to combat AI-generated misinformation.
- **AI and Social Media Companies:** Required to comply with the new labeling rules.
- **Users:** Must declare whether their content is AI-generated.
- **Technical Systems Developers:** Tasked with implementing verification measures.

###  Technical Details
- **Visual Media Labeling:** Platforms must label AI-generated visual media with a marker covering at least 10% of the image.
- **Audio Clips Labeling:** Audio clips must have an identifier during the first 10% of playback.
- **User Declaration:** Users must declare whether their content is AI-generated.
- **Technical Verification:** Companies must implement technical systems for verification.

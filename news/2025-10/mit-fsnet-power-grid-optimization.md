# MIT Develops FSNet to Optimize Power Grid Operations with Machine Learning

**Source:** news.mit.edu  
**Date:** 2025-10-15  
**URL:** [https://news.mit.edu/topic/machine-learning?utm_source=openai](https://news.mit.edu/topic/machine-learning?utm_source=openai)

MIT researchers have developed FSNet, an AI-based system that rapidly finds feasible solutions for optimizing electricity flow in power grids. This innovation aims to improve grid efficiency and reliability by leveraging machine learning techniques to optimize the complex, often nonlinear power grid operations.

FSNet facilitates faster decision-making for energy distribution, which has potential benefits in energy conservation and operational cost reduction, contributing to more sustainable and resilient power infrastructure.

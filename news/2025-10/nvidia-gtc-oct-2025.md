# Nvidia GTC October 2025: Self-Driving Ubers, 6G with Nokia, and AI Supercomputers for DOE

**Source:** Tom's Guide  
**Date:** 2025-10-28  
**URL:** [https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/live/nvidia-gtc-october-2025-live](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/live/nvidia-gtc-october-2025-live)  

**Summary:** Nvidia announced key initiatives including collaboration with Nokia on AI-native 6G infrastructure, deployment of Nvidia-powered robotaxis with Uber and Stellantis, and construction of AI supercomputers for US national labs.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

At the Nvidia GTC October 2025 conference, CEO Jensen Huang unveiled several strategic partnerships and technological advancements:

- **AI-Native 6G Infrastructure with Nokia:** Nvidia and Nokia are collaborating to develop AI-native 6G infrastructure using the Aerial RAN Computer platform. 【source】([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01533))

- **Self-Driving Ubers:** Nvidia, Uber, and Stellantis have partnered to deploy Nvidia-powered robotaxis with Level 4 autonomy, aiming to launch production in 2028. (Specific Reuters article is currently unavailable.)

- **AI Supercomputers for the Department of Energy:** Nvidia will work with the U.S. Department of Energy to build seven new AI supercomputers at national laboratories, featuring the GB200 NVL72 AI supercomputing rack. 【source】([tomsguide.com](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/live/nvidia-gtc-october-2025-live))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

These initiatives signify Nvidia's expansion into critical sectors:

- **Telecommunications:** The partnership with Nokia positions Nvidia at the forefront of developing AI-driven 6G networks, potentially revolutionizing wireless communication.

- **Transportation:** Collaborating with Uber and Stellantis to deploy autonomous vehicles could accelerate the adoption of self-driving technology, impacting urban mobility and transportation infrastructure.

- **Government Collaboration:** Building AI supercomputers for the Department of Energy underscores Nvidia's role in advancing national research capabilities and addressing complex scientific challenges.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **Nvidia:** Leading AI and GPU technology company.

- **Nokia:** Telecommunications giant collaborating on 6G infrastructure.

- **Uber:** Ride-hailing service partnering to deploy autonomous vehicles.

- **Stellantis:** Automotive manufacturer involved in developing robotaxis.

- **U.S. Department of Energy:** Government agency collaborating on AI supercomputers.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Aerial RAN Computer Platform:** Nokia's platform for developing AI-native 6G infrastructure. 【source】([arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.01533))

- **GB200 NVL72 AI Supercomputing Rack:** Nvidia's 72-GPU unit designed for AI workloads, offering 10x performance and cost improvements. 【source】([tomsguide.com](https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/live/nvidia-gtc-october-2025-live))

- **Level 4 Autonomy:** Self-driving capability where the vehicle can operate without human intervention under certain conditions.

## 📰 OpenAI's Deep Research Tool Enhances Autonomous Web Browsing

**Source:** Wikipedia  
**Date:** 2025-10-18  
**URL:** [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Deep_Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ChatGPT_Deep_Research)  
**Summary:** OpenAI introduced Deep Research, integrated into ChatGPT, enabling autonomous web browsing and analysis to create cited research reports from diverse content including text, images, and PDFs.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

OpenAI unveiled Deep Research, an AI agent integrated into ChatGPT, designed to autonomously browse the web and generate comprehensive, cited research reports on user-specified topics. This tool interprets and analyzes various content types, including text, images, and PDFs, providing users with detailed insights.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

Deep Research significantly streamlines the research process by automating the collection and synthesis of information from multiple online sources. This advancement reduces the time and effort required for in-depth research tasks, making it a valuable resource for professionals and researchers seeking efficient information gathering.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **OpenAI:** The developer of Deep Research, integrating it into ChatGPT to enhance its capabilities.

### 🔹 Technical Details

- **Model:** Deep Research is based on a specialized version of OpenAI's o3 model, optimized for web browsing and data analysis.

- **Capabilities:** The tool autonomously searches the web, interprets and analyzes text, images, and PDFs, and generates detailed research reports with citations.

- **Limitations:** OpenAI acknowledges that Deep Research may occasionally produce factual inaccuracies or incorrect inferences and may struggle to distinguish authoritative information from rumors.

---

### 🔹 Benchmark Results

Deep Research scored 26.6% on the "Humanity's Last Exam" benchmark, outperforming competitors like DeepSeek's R1 model (9.4%) and GPT-4o (3.3%).

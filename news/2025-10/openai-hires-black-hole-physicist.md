## OpenAI Hires Black Hole Physicist Alex Lupsasca in Broader Science Initiative

**Source:** Axios  
**Date:** October 16, 2025  
**URL:** [https://axios.com/2025/10/16/openai-black-hole-physicist-science](https://axios.com/2025/10/16/openai-black-hole-physicist-science)  
**Summary:** OpenAI has expanded its scientific research efforts by hiring Alex Lupsasca, a theoretical physicist known for his work on black holes, aiming to bolster its credibility beyond AI tools.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

OpenAI has hired Alex Lupsasca, a prize-winning theoretical physicist specializing in black holes, to join its new science team. Lupsasca is the first member of the "OpenAI for Science" initiative, led by Kevin Weil. He will continue his role as a professor at Vanderbilt University while contributing to OpenAI's scientific endeavors.

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This move signifies OpenAI's commitment to deepening its scientific credibility beyond its reputation for AI tools like ChatGPT and video applications. By integrating experts from various scientific fields, OpenAI aims to position itself as a significant player in scientific discovery, competing with other advanced AI research labs.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- Alex Lupsasca: A Romanian-born theoretical physicist known for his research on black holes. He has been awarded the New Horizons in Physics Breakthrough Prize for his work on the photon ring and its detection in future experiments.

- Kevin Weil: Vice President of AI for Science at OpenAI, leading the "OpenAI for Science" initiative.

- Vanderbilt University: Lupsasca's academic institution, where he will continue his professorship.

### Technical Details

- OpenAI for Science Initiative: A new program aimed at developing AI systems to support scientific reasoning and accelerate discoveries in physics and mathematics.

- GPT-5 Pro: OpenAI's advanced AI model that Lupsasca has utilized to solve complex astrophysics problems, demonstrating AI's potential in scientific research.

# 📰 Anthropic to invest $50 billion to build AI data centers in US

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** 2025-11-12  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/technology/anthropic-invest-50-billion-build-data-centers-us-2025-11-12/?utm_source=openai](https://www.reuters.com/technology/anthropic-invest-50-billion-build-data-centers-us-2025-11-12/?utm_source=openai)  

AI startup Anthropic announced a $50 billion investment to build custom data centers in Texas and New York. This initiative aims to address the growing demand for AI capabilities and infrastructure in the US. The project is expected to create thousands of jobs by 2026, contributing significantly to both technological advancement and economic growth.

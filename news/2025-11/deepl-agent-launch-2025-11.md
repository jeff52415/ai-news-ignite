##  DeepL Launches DeepL Agent for Automated Task Management

**Source:** aiagentstore.ai  
**Date:** November 12, 2025  
**URL:** [https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week?utm_source=openai](https://aiagentstore.ai/ai-agent-news/this-week?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** DeepL releases DeepL Agent, an AI coworker designed to automate repetitive tasks for knowledge workers, completing over 20,000 tasks in beta testing, aimed at increasing productivity.

---

###  What Happened

DeepL, a global AI product and research company, has officially launched DeepL Agent, an autonomous AI coworker designed to streamline and automate a wide range of tasks for knowledge workers. After extensive beta testing, during which over 20,000 tasks were completed, DeepL Agent is now generally available.  

###  Why It Matters

The introduction of DeepL Agent signifies a significant advancement in AI-driven productivity tools. By automating repetitive and time-consuming tasks, it allows knowledge workers to focus on more strategic and creative aspects of their roles, potentially transforming workplace efficiency across various industries.  

###  Who's Involved

- **DeepL**: The company behind DeepL Agent, known for its expertise in language AI and translation services.  
- **Jarek Kutylowski**: CEO and Founder of DeepL, who emphasized the company's vision to unlock human potential by transforming work across languages and beyond.  

###  Technical Details

- **DeepL Agent**: An autonomous AI coworker designed to automate a broad range of tasks for knowledge workers. It operates within users' digital environments, responding to natural language commands to carry out complex workflows safely and independently.

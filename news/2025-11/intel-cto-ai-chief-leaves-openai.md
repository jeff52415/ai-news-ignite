## Intel's CTO and AI Chief Leaves for OpenAI After Seven Months

**Source:** Tom's Hardware  
**Date:** November 11, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/intels-cto-and-ai-chief-leaves-for-openai-after-just-seven-months-in-role-follows-another-ai-data-center-exec-out-the-door-lip-bu-tan-to-take-up-the-slack](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/intels-cto-and-ai-chief-leaves-for-openai-after-just-seven-months-in-role-follows-another-ai-data-center-exec-out-the-door-lip-bu-tan-to-take-up-the-slack)  
**Summary:** Intel's Chief Technology Officer and AI head, Sachin Katti, departed after seven months to join OpenAI as head of compute infrastructure, highlighting shifts in AI leadership.

---

### What Happened

Sachin Katti, Intel's Chief Technology Officer and head of AI, has left the company after seven months to join OpenAI as head of compute infrastructure. This move follows the departure of Saurabh Kulkarni, Intel's Vice President of Data Center AI Product Management, who reportedly joined AMD. In response, Intel's CEO, Lip-Bu Tan, will personally oversee the AI and Advanced Technologies divisions. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/intel-ceo-oversee-companys-ai-efforts-after-departure-exec-openai-2025-11-10/?utm_source=openai))

### Why It Matters

Katti's departure underscores the competitive nature of AI talent acquisition, with leading tech companies vying for expertise to advance their AI initiatives. Intel's restructuring efforts, including leadership changes and strategic realignments, aim to strengthen its position in the AI sector. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/business/intel-ceo-oversee-companys-ai-efforts-after-departure-exec-openai-2025-11-10/?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved

- **Sachin Katti:** Former Chief Technology Officer and head of AI at Intel, now joining OpenAI as head of compute infrastructure.
- **Lip-Bu Tan:** CEO of Intel, who will oversee the AI and Advanced Technologies divisions following Katti's departure.
- **Saurabh Kulkarni:** Former Vice President of Data Center AI Product Management at Intel, reportedly joining AMD.
- **Greg Brockman:** President and co-founder of OpenAI, who welcomed Katti to the team.

### Technical Details

- **Intel's AI and Advanced Technologies Groups:** Divisions focusing on AI research, development, and product strategies.
- **OpenAI's Compute Infrastructure:** The foundational hardware and systems supporting OpenAI's artificial general intelligence (AGI) research and applications.

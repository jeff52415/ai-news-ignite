## Microsoft Unveils Its First "AI Superfactory"

**Source:** TechRadar  
**Date:** 2025-11-20  
**URL:** [https://www.techradar.com/pro/microsoft-unveils-its-first-ai-superfactory-heres-what-the-future-of-computing-will-really-look-like](https://www.techradar.com/pro/microsoft-unveils-its-first-ai-superfactory-heres-what-the-future-of-computing-will-really-look-like)  
**Summary:** Microsoft has launched its first "AI superfactory," connecting massive datacenters in Wisconsin and Atlanta via a high-speed, dedicated fiber network. This infrastructure is designed to support large-scale AI model training, featuring advanced cooling systems and optimized hardware configurations.

---

### What Happened

Microsoft unveiled its first "AI superfactory," a network of interconnected datacenters in Wisconsin and Atlanta, designed to accelerate large-scale AI model training. This initiative aims to create a distributed system functioning as a virtual supercomputer, capable of handling complex AI workloads across multiple sites.

### Why It Matters

The AI superfactory represents a significant advancement in AI infrastructure, addressing the growing demand for computational power required by modern AI models. By connecting multiple datacenters, Microsoft can pool resources, reduce latency, and enhance the efficiency of AI model training, potentially leading to faster and more effective AI developments.

### Who's Involved

- **Microsoft:** Spearheading the development and deployment of the AI superfactory.
- **NVIDIA:** Providing the GB200 NVL72 rack units with Blackwell GPUs integral to the system's hardware.
- **OpenAI:** Utilizing the infrastructure for training advanced AI models.

### Technical Details

- **Datacenter Locations:** Wisconsin and Atlanta.
- **Network Infrastructure:** A dedicated AI Wide Area Network (AI WAN) spanning over 120,000 miles of fiber-optic cables, facilitating high-speed data transfer between sites.
- **Hardware:** NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 rack units equipped with Blackwell GPUs.
- **Cooling System:** Advanced liquid cooling technology designed to efficiently manage heat and reduce water usage.
- **Design Architecture:** Two-story datacenter design to maximize GPU density and minimize latency.

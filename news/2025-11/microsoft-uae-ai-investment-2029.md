## Microsoft's $15.2 Billion Investment in UAE AI Data Centers by 2029

**Source:** Jason Wade  
**Date:** 2025-11-04  
**URL:** [https://www.jasonwade.com/today-s-ai-tech-news-november-4-2025?utm_source=openai](https://www.jasonwade.com/today-s-ai-tech-news-november-4-2025?utm_source=openai)  
**Summary:** Microsoft plans to invest heavily in AI infrastructure in the UAE, including seeking US approval for exporting advanced Nvidia chips, signaling a major AI expansion in the Middle East.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

Microsoft announced a substantial $15.2 billion investment in the United Arab Emirates (UAE) over a seven-year period ending in 2029. This initiative focuses on expanding artificial intelligence (AI) and cloud computing infrastructure in the region. A significant component of this investment includes the deployment of advanced Nvidia GPUs to power AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft's own services. The U.S. government has granted Microsoft export licenses to ship these advanced Nvidia chips to the UAE, facilitating the establishment of AI data centers. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/microsoft-invest-over-15-billion-uae-secures-us-export-licenses-ai-chips-2025-11-03/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

This investment underscores Microsoft's commitment to bolstering AI capabilities in the Middle East, aligning with the UAE's ambition to become a global AI hub. The collaboration with G42, a UAE-based AI company, and the establishment of AI data centers are expected to enhance the region's technological infrastructure and foster innovation. Additionally, the export of advanced Nvidia chips to the UAE highlights the strategic importance of the region in global AI development. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/microsoft-invest-over-15-billion-uae-secures-us-export-licenses-ai-chips-2025-11-03/?utm_source=openai))

### Who's Involved

- **Microsoft Corporation**: Leading the investment and expansion of AI infrastructure in the UAE.
- **G42**: A UAE-based AI company in which Microsoft has acquired a $1.5 billion equity stake to co-develop advanced AI solutions. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/microsoft-g42-announce-200-mw-data-centre-capacity-expansion-uae-2025-11-05/?utm_source=openai))
- **U.S. Government**: Granted export licenses for advanced Nvidia GPUs to support the establishment of AI data centers in the UAE. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/microsoft-invest-over-15-billion-uae-secures-us-export-licenses-ai-chips-2025-11-03/?utm_source=openai))
- **Nvidia Corporation**: Supplying advanced GPUs, including A100, H100, H200, and GB300 models, for the AI data centers. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/microsoft-invest-over-15-billion-uae-secures-us-export-licenses-ai-chips-2025-11-03/?utm_source=openai))

### Technical Details

- **Investment Amount**: $15.2 billion over seven years, with $7.3 billion already invested by the end of 2025 and an additional $7.9 billion planned from 2026 to 2029. ([blogs.microsoft.com](https://blogs.microsoft.com/on-the-issues/2025/11/03/microsofts-15-2-billion-usd-investment-in-the-uae/?utm_source=openai))
- **AI and Cloud Infrastructure**: Expansion of AI and cloud data centers in the UAE, including a 200-megawatt capacity expansion through Khazna Data Centers, a G42 subsidiary. ([news.microsoft.com](https://news.microsoft.com/source/emea/2025/11/microsoft-and-g42-accelerate-uaes-digital-future-with-major-data-centre-expansion/?utm_source=openai))
- **Nvidia GPUs**: Deployment of over 60,000 advanced Nvidia GPUs, including A100, H100, H200, and GB300 models, to power AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, and Microsoft. ([apnews.com](https://apnews.com/article/12ae42b88f4c5fec3cbbff3b4bd1ac0c?utm_source=openai))

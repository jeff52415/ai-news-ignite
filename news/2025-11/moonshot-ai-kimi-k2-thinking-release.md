# Moonshot AI Releases Kimi K2 Thinking for Advanced Reasoning

**Source:** en.wikipedia.org  
**Date:** 2025-11-12  
**URL:** [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonshot_AI?utm_source=openai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moonshot_AI?utm_source=openai)  

Moonshot AI has released Kimi K2 Thinking, an open-source AI model featuring a 1-trillion-parameter Mixture of Experts (MoE) architecture. This model supports extensive context lengths designed to enable advanced reasoning and perform complex agentic tasks, pushing the boundaries of AI capabilities.

## 📰 President Trump Advocates for Unified Federal AI Regulation

**Source:** Reuters  
**Date:** November 18, 2025  
**URL:** [https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-warns-against-ai-overregulation-says-us-needs-have-one-federal-standard-2025-11-18/](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-warns-against-ai-overregulation-says-us-needs-have-one-federal-standard-2025-11-18/)  
**Summary:** President Trump emphasized the need for a single federal AI regulation standard to avoid fragmented state laws that could hinder U.S. competitiveness with China.

---

### 🔹 What Happened

On November 18, 2025, President Donald Trump called for a unified federal standard to regulate artificial intelligence (AI) in the United States. He warned that state-level overregulation could undermine the nation's AI development and allow China to surpass the U.S. in the global AI race. Trump urged lawmakers to incorporate this federal standard into the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) or pass it as a separate bill. ([reuters.com](https://www.reuters.com/world/trump-warns-against-ai-overregulation-says-us-needs-have-one-federal-standard-2025-11-18/?utm_source=openai))

### 🔹 Why It Matters

Establishing a single federal standard for AI regulation is crucial to maintain the U.S.'s competitive edge in AI technology. Fragmented state laws could create inconsistencies and hinder innovation, potentially allowing other nations, particularly China, to lead in AI advancements. A unified approach aims to streamline AI development and deployment across the country.

### 🔹 Who's Involved

- **President Donald Trump**: Advocating for a unified federal AI regulation standard.
- **U.S. Congress**: Encouraged to include the federal standard in the NDAA or pass it as a separate bill.

### 🔹 Technical Details

The proposed federal standard aims to:

- Prevent overregulation by individual states.
- Ensure consistent AI policies across the nation.
- Maintain U.S. competitiveness in the global AI race.

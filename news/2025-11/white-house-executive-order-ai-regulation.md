# White House Considers Executive Order to Standardize AI Regulations Across States

**Source:** Axios  
**Date:** 2025-11-19  
**URL:** [https://www.axios.com/2025/11/19/trump-ai-state-laws-executive-order?utm_source=openai](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/19/trump-ai-state-laws-executive-order?utm_source=openai)

## Summary

The White House is considering an executive order aimed at overriding the patchwork of AI regulations imposed by individual states through a unified federal policy. This move intends to eliminate regulatory fragmentation across states, thereby easing compliance burdens for AI developers and companies. The executive order would establish standardized regulations to foster consistent oversight, innovation, and safe deployment of AI technologies nationwide.

This federal intervention seeks to balance the rapid advancement of AI with clear, cohesive governance standards rather than a fragmented approach that could hinder progress and complicate legal compliance for organizations operating in multiple states.
